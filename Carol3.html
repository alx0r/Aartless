📋 Project Overview

What We're Building

We are creating a complete digital and business presence for Carol Crous, transforming 15+ years of educational consulting expertise into a scalable, multi-faceted online business. This inclusive strategy combines a robust digital foundation (websites, social media, content) with an expanded portfolio of services including tutoring and specialized travel management. The goal is to build a comprehensive brand that helps international families navigate complex educational and lifestyle transitions worldwide.

Why This Project Matters

🌍 Global Reach

Carol's expertise spans 6 countries. A digital presence allows her to help families anywhere, breaking geographical limitations.

📈 Scalable Income

Moving from 1-on-1 consulting to a mix of digital products, online courses, and specialized services means Carol can help more families while building sustainable, diversified income streams.

🎯 Targeted Marketing

A strong digital presence ensures that international families actively searching for educational consultants, tutors, or travel support find Carol when they need help most.

Primary Target Markets

  • 🇪🇺
    Western European Expatriate Families
    Families relocating within Europe or to Turkey, Netherlands, UK who need guidance on international school systems and transitions.
  • 🇿🇦
    South African Families
    Families seeking international education opportunities, leveraging Carol's deep understanding of both SA and international systems.
  • 🏢

    Employees of international companies moving with children who need expert guidance on school placement.
  • 🧠
    Parents of Children with Learning Differences

  • 🗺️
    Families Seeking Enriched Experiences
    Parents looking for premium services like private tutoring, curated holiday camps, and educational family travel.

⚙️ Technical & Operational Implementation

This is the technical and operational blueprint for transforming Carol's expertise into a thriving business.

Phase 1: Digital Foundation (Weeks 1-2)

Goal: Establish a professional and consistent digital identity.

Step 1: Secure Digital Assets
  • Finalize brand name and secure the corresponding domain name.
  • Set up professional email: carol@[chosen domain].
  • Create and secure social media handles on LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook.
Step 2: Professional Branding & Business Model
  • Conduct professional photoshoot for headshots and marketing materials.
  • Develop platform-specific bios that communicate the full range of services.
  • Finalize the pricing structure and package deals for all service offerings.

Phase 2: Content Hub & Systems (Weeks 3-4)

Goal: Build the central website and set up the tools needed for efficient operation.

Website Development

Build a simple, professional, mobile-first website showcasing services, expertise, testimonials, and a clear contact method. This will be our central hub.

Operational Tools Setup
  • Canva Pro: For creating all branded graphics and marketing materials.
  • Calendly/Acuity: For easy scheduling of consultations.
  • Mailchimp/ConvertKit: To start building an email list for newsletters and promotions.
  • Google Business Profile: To capture local search traffic in Istanbul.

Phase 3: Content & Product Launch (Weeks 5-8)

Goal: Begin consistent content creation and launch initial digital products.

Content Calendar & Creation

Develop a 3-month content plan and begin creating content in batches. Focus on providing value and showcasing expertise across all platforms.

Digital Product Development

Create initial "lead magnet" freebies (e.g., relocation checklist) and low-cost digital products (e.g., a detailed guide to choosing an IB school) to generate leads and passive income.
