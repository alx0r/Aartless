About Me

I am a passionate Cross-Cultural Education Experience Designer who bridges the gap between diverse educational systems and international student success. With my unique blend of design thinking, TEFL expertise, and cultural adaptability, I create transformative learning experiences that help students and families navigate international school transitions with confidence.

My approach combines visual design principles with educational psychology to develop culturally-responsive materials, digital orientation programs, and comprehensive transition strategies. As an energetic, intuitive professional with deep cultural awareness, I specialize in turning the complex challenges of international education transitions into seamless, engaging experiences that set students up for long-term success.
