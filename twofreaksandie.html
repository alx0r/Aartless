🌱 Why This Page Exists (The Smart Pod Manifesto)

You might be wondering why I have my own separate page instead of just posting on Instagram or TikTok. Here's the thing: this is MY digital home, and that matters more than you might think.

🏠 Ownership & Independence

This space is MINE. No algorithm decides what you see. No platform can delete it overnight. No billionaire can change the rules on me. When you visit this page, you're getting exactly what I want to share—unfiltered, unmediated, unmonetized (unless I choose otherwise).
I update on MY schedule. Not when an algorithm rewards me. Not when engagement is "optimal." When I have something to say, when I've made something cool, when there's a show to announce—that's when this page changes. It's real in a way social media can never be.

♿ Accessibility First

Works offline. Save this page to your device and you can access it anytime, anywhere—no internet needed. Perfect for South Africa's load shedding reality, for people with limited data, or for anyone who just wants information that STAYS PUT.
Screen reader friendly. Proper semantic HTML means people using assistive technology can actually navigate this page. Social media platforms? Often a nightmare for accessibility. This? Built with care.
Low bandwidth. No tracking scripts, no heavy ads, no unnecessary bloat. This page loads fast even on slow connections because it respects your data limits and your time.

🛡️ Anti-Platform Politics

No surveillance capitalism. There are no tracking cookies here. No data harvesting. No selling your attention to advertisers. You read what I write, I don't know who you are, and that's exactly how it should be.
Platform-independent. Instagram could ban me tomorrow. TikTok could shut down. Twitter could become... whatever it's becoming. This page? Still here. My community can always find me because I OWN this space.
No engagement manipulation. I'm not optimizing for likes. Not chasing viral moments. Not performing for an algorithm. This is just me, sharing what matters to me, in a format that respects both of us.

✨ Bespoke & Authentic

Designed to reflect ME. Every color, every font, every little decorative scissor emoji—it's all chosen to feel like my actual creative space. Social media gives everyone the same template. This? This is MINE.
Living document. This page grows with me. New projects, new interests, new thoughts—I can add sections whenever I want. Try doing that in an Instagram bio.
Archival & permanent. Everything I post here stays here (unless I decide to change it). No disappearing stories, no expired content, no "this post is no longer available." It's a real record of my creative journey.

🤝 Ethical Tech

Open web principles. Built with standard HTML, CSS, and minimal JavaScript. Anyone can view the source code and see exactly how it works. No mystery boxes, no proprietary nonsense.
Sustainable digital practice. Low energy consumption, no server-intensive processes, designed to last. This is digital minimalism that actually respects resources.
Community over metrics. Success here isn't measured in followers or engagement rates. It's measured in real connections, actual conversations, and whether this space helps me share my work authentically.

This is what the indie web looks like.

Small, personal, owned by the person who made it. Not because it's trendy, but because it's RIGHT.

— Andie ✨
