🚀 Setup in 20 Minutes (Really!)

If you can send an email, you can set up ShiBaby. No coding, no technical skills needed.

2 minutes

Get Your Free Template

Download our ready-to-use ShiBaby template or ask any AI to create one for you.

// Option 1: Download template wget https://shibaby.com/template.html // Option 2: Ask AI (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) "Create a ShiBaby HTML document for my 6-month-old named Alex"

5 minutes

Create Free Account

Sign up for GitHub (it's like Google Drive for websites). 100% free, no credit card.

  • Go to github.com
  • Click "Sign up"
  • Choose username (e.g., "family-baby-care")
  • Verify your email
3
8 minutes

Upload & Activate

Paste your template and turn it into a live website with one click.

  • Create new "repository" (just a folder)
  • Paste your ShiBaby HTML code
  • Click "Settings" → "Pages" → "Save"
  • Your site goes live instantly
4
5 minutes

Customize for Your Baby

Add your baby's name, birth details, and family information.
