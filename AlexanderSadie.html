Our Love Story

A tale of serendipity, vintage books, and endless conversations

It was a rainy October afternoon in 2019 when Alexander first noticed Sadie in the corner of Blackwell's bookshop in Oxford. She was absorbed in a worn copy of Tennyson's poetry, her fingers tracing the gilded edges with the kind of reverence reserved for sacred things. He'd come looking for a first edition Dickens; he left with her phone number scrawled on a bookmark.

Their first proper date was a walking tour of Victorian architecture through Notting Hill. What was meant to be a two-hour afternoon stretched into twilight, then dinner, then drinks at a hidden speakeasy where jazz played and the world felt suspended in amber light. They talked about everything: the romance of forgotten train stations, the poetry of overgrown gardens, the beauty in things that refuse to be modern.

Three years of London adventures followed—weekend escapes to the countryside, antique market Sundays, candlelit dinners in their tiny flat where Sadie would cook while Alexander read to her from whatever book had captured his imagination that week. They built a life around the small, beautiful things: fresh flowers on Monday mornings, handwritten notes, vinyl records playing while rain drummed against the windows.

In June of 2024, Alexander proposed in the same bookshop where they'd met. He'd conspired with the owner to create an entire display of Sadie's favorite poets, with a custom-bound volume at the center containing a single question written in calligraphy: "Will you marry me?" She said yes before he'd even finished kneeling. The other patrons applauded. The rain outside turned to sunshine.

~ Together since October 12, 2019 ~

Wedding Details

An intimate celebration in the English countryside

The Venue

Willowmere Estate is a 19th-century manor house nestled in the heart of the Cotswolds. With its honey-stone façade, wisteria-draped terraces, and sprawling English gardens, it embodies the romantic elegance of a bygone era. The ceremony will take place in the estate's private rose garden, surrounded by climbing vines and century-old trees. The reception follows in the Victorian conservatory, where glass walls blur the line between indoors and out, and string lights will transform the space into something magical as dusk falls.

Dress Code

Bohemian Victorian Elegance

Think romantic, whimsical, and gently vintage. We love the idea of flowing fabrics, rich jewel tones (burgundy, forest green, deep plum), lace details, and touches of gold. Ladies might consider midi or maxi dresses with vintage-inspired silhouettes. Gentlemen, we adore the look of well-tailored suits in earthy tones, waistcoats, bow ties, and the occasional pocket watch.

This is the countryside in June, so you'll want to consider the grass and garden pathways—perhaps save the stilettos for another occasion. Most importantly, wear something that makes you feel beautiful and comfortable enough to dance under the stars.

Weather & What to Bring

June in the Cotswolds is usually glorious, but England being England, we recommend bringing:

  • A light wrap or shawl — Evenings can be cool, especially in the garden
  • Comfortable shoes — The estate has gravel paths and grassy areas
  • An umbrella — Just in case (we'll have vintage parasols available too!)
  • Your dancing shoes — The party goes late

Weekend Schedule

A celebration spanning three magical days

Friday, June 12 • 7:00 PM
Welcome Drinks
Informal gathering at The Wild Rabbit pub in Kingham (15 minutes from Willowmere). Come as you are, meet fellow guests, and start the weekend with local ales and good company. No need to dress up—this is purely about connection and anticipation.
Saturday, June 13 • 2:00 PM
Garden Tea Party
A Victorian-inspired afternoon tea in the gardens at Willowmere. Think finger sandwiches, scones with clotted cream, delicate pastries, and endless pots of tea. Lawn games will be set up (croquet, anyone?), and we'll have a live string quartet. This is optional but highly encouraged—it's our favorite kind of slow, leisurely afternoon.
Sunday, June 14 • 3:00 PM
Ceremony Begins
Please arrive by 2:30 PM. The ceremony will take place in the Rose Garden, beneath a floral arch we've been dreaming about for months. Seating is a mix of vintage chairs and benches—choose your spot and settle in. We'll keep it short, sweet, and hopefully not too tear-inducing (no promises).
Sunday, June 14 • 4:00 PM
Champagne Reception
Immediately following the ceremony, join us on the South Terrace for champagne, canapés, and golden hour photographs. Live acoustic music, garden views, and the beginning of what we hope will be an unforgettable evening.
Sunday, June 14 • 6:00 PM
Dinner & Toasts
A seated dinner in the Victorian Conservatory. Expect a feast: locally sourced ingredients, family-style service, botanical-inspired tablescapes, and toasts from our favorite people. The menu is seasonal British with a modern twist—think roasted game, heritage vegetables, artisan cheeses, and a dessert table you won't forget.
Sunday, June 14 • 9:00 PM
First Dance & Dancing
Our first dance will be followed by live band performances until midnight. We've curated a mix of vintage swing, indie folk, and the occasional modern crowd-pleaser. The dance floor will be open under string lights and stars. This is the part where shoes come off and inhibitions disappear.
Sunday, June 14 • Midnight
Late Night Lounge
For those not ready to call it a night, we'll have a cozy lounge area with vintage cocktails, a vinyl DJ set, and comfortable seating by the fireplace in the Manor's Library. Cigars and whiskey on the terrace for anyone interested.
Monday, June 15 • 10:00 AM
Farewell Brunch
A casual farewell brunch at Willowmere for anyone still around. Full English breakfast, pastries, strong coffee, and the chance to relive last night's best moments before we all reluctantly return to reality.

Travel & Accommodations

Getting to the Cotswolds and where to stay

Getting There

By Train: London to Kingham station takes approximately 90 minutes from Paddington. We recommend the 12:30 PM train on Friday if you're attending welcome drinks. From Kingham, it's a 15-minute taxi ride to Willowmere Estate.

By Car: From London, it's about a 2-hour drive via the M40. Parking is available at Willowmere Estate and most accommodation options. The countryside drive is stunning—roll the windows down.

By Air: International guests can fly into London Heathrow (2 hours to Cotswolds) or Birmingham Airport (1 hour). We recommend hiring a car if you want to explore the area.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Can I bring a date or plus-one? +
Due to venue capacity, we're only able to accommodate guests formally listed on your invitation. If your invitation includes "and guest," then absolutely! If you're unsure, please reach out to us directly.
Are children welcome? +
We adore your little ones, but we've decided to make this an adults-only celebration. We hope this gives parents a chance to relax and enjoy themselves. Children of immediate family members are of course welcome.
What if I have dietary restrictions? +
Please let us know via the RSVP form, and we'll work with our catering team to accommodate you. Our menu includes vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options, and we can handle most allergies with advance notice.
Will the wedding be indoors or outdoors? +
The ceremony will be outdoors in the Rose Garden (weather permitting—we have a gorgeous backup plan in the Conservatory). The reception will be in the glass-enclosed Victorian Conservatory, which gives you the best of both worlds: garden views with climate control.
What time should I arrive? +
Please arrive by 2:30 PM on Sunday, June 14th. The ceremony begins promptly at 3:00 PM. We recommend building in extra time for Cotswolds traffic and finding parking.
Is there parking at the venue? +
Yes! Willowmere Estate has ample parking. Staff will direct you to designated areas upon arrival. If you're staying at one of our hotel blocks, we highly recommend taking the complimentary shuttle service—no need to worry about driving after the celebration.
Can I take photos during the ceremony? +
We're having an "unplugged" ceremony—we kindly ask that phones and cameras stay tucked away so everyone can be fully present. Our incredible photographer will capture every moment, and we promise to share photos afterward! Once the ceremony ends, snap away to your heart's content.
What about gifts? +
Your presence is truly the only present we need. If you'd like to give a gift, we'd be grateful for contributions toward our honeymoon adventure through Scotland and Ireland. Details can be found at our registry page: alexanderandsadie.com/registry
Will there be transportation from hotels? +
Yes! We're providing complimentary shuttle service from all hotel blocks to Willowmere Estate for all weekend events. Detailed schedules will be emailed to guests closer to the date. Return shuttles will run until 1:00 AM on Sunday night.
What if I need to contact you? +
For any questions, please email us at: hello@alexanderandsadie.com
