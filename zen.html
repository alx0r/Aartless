Pre-Retreat Preparation

Welcome to your Alpine Awakening journey. This living portal will be your guide before, during, and after our retreat together.

What to Bring

  • Yoga mat (we provide extras if needed)
  • Comfortable clothing for practice and hiking
  • Warm layers for alpine evenings
  • Journal and pen for reflections
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Hiking shoes or sturdy sneakers

Recommended Reading

Deepen your practice with these selections available in our retreat library:

  • The Yoga of Breath by Richard Rosen
  • Moving into Stillness by Erich Schiffmann
  • The Untethered Soul by Michael A. Singer
