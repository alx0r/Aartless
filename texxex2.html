⚙️ Behind the Sound

Daisy Arson is no longer a solo act. This is a duo. This is a transformation.

DAISY ARSON

Role: Vocals, Songwriting

Departure from pop roots. Harsh vocals influenced by riot grrrl pioneers. Impressive control switching between vocal styles.

Vision: Raw, authentic, unfiltered rage channeled into art.

JOUSKA

Role: Production, Sound Design

Local music influencer and seasoned producer. Brings clean, well-layered production that maintains a distinctive edge.

Philosophy: "Not bound by genre. Just want it to sound cool."

🎬 INSPIRATIONS

The horror movies, the music, the movements that shaped D.O.A.

HORROR CINEMA

D.O.A was directly inspired by horror movie aesthetics—the visceral fear, the raw terror, the feeling of being hunted. We wanted to capture that same intensity sonically.

RIOT GRRRL MOVEMENT

Bikini Kill. Sleater-Kinney. Le Tigre. The original feminists who screamed their rage into microphones. This track stands on their shoulders.

INDUSTRIAL PIONEERS

Nine Inch Nails. Ministry. The aggressive electronics, the layered distortion, the controlled chaos. Jouska's production pulls from this lineage.
