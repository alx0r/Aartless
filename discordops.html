Executive Summary

This document provides a compact, actionable operations manual for the alimcforever Discord newsroom. It defines governance, moderation workflows, content handling, incident response, and publication ethics in a mobile-first, print-optimized single-file (FFE-compliant) reference for staff and collaborators.

  • Audience: moderators, ops, content collaborators.
  • Primary aims: preserve a calm research environment, protect participant privacy, ensure legal compliance, and maintain editorial integrity.
  • Legal frame: governed under England & Wales jurisdiction and platform Terms of Service.

Context

alimcforever functions as a small, moderated digital newsroom: a research-first "third space" for collective thinking and shared learning. The community values collaborative creativity (scenius) over individual amplification. This operations guide exists to ensure consistent, transparent moderation and content practices.

Methodology

Operational Principles

  • Least intrusive enforcement: prefer education and correction over punitive actions when appropriate.
  • Preserve conversation context: archive evidence, avoid deleting content when investigating unless legally required.
  • Privacy-first: never expose member identities; escalate via secure channels.
  • Transparency: record enforcement actions in the mod log with basic rationale and timestamps.

Recordkeeping & Evidence

Maintain a private moderation log (encrypted or access-limited). Each record should include:

  • UTC timestamp (ISO8601), channel, message IDs or screenshots reference.
  • Action taken, moderator ID (role), and short rationale.
  • Escalation status and links to saved evidence.

Moderation & Incident Response

Moderation Ethos

Moderation aims to protect the research space and its participants. Approach all incidents with impartiality, proportionality, and documentation.

Incident Severity Tiers

  1. Tier 1 — Low (clarify / guide): off-topic posts, minor spam, tone issues. Action: warning & guidance, pin reference to rules.
  2. Tier 2 — Medium (restrict): repeated rule breaches, targeted harassment, privacy concerns. Action: temporary mute, role restriction, require verification.
  3. Tier 3 — High (escalate): doxxing, illicit requests, sustained brigading, platform-policy violations. Action: immediate removal, preserve evidence, report to platform.

Incident Response Checklist

  • 1) Contain: limit the actor's ability to continue (mute, restrict roles).
  • 2) Preserve: save message IDs, channel, screenshots, and any attachments in the private incident folder.
  • 3) Assess: evaluate against rules and platform ToS; determine severity.
  • 4) Act: apply sanctions per tiered guidance; document action in the mod log.
  • 5) Notify: inform moderator team and owner channel (no public announcements unless required).
  • 6) Escalate: if criminal or platform policy concerns, submit evidence to the platform Trust & Safety team and local authorities as needed.

Appeals & Review

Provide a clear appeals path: members may request a review through the support channel. Moderators must log appeal intake and timestamps; reviews should be completed within a reasonable internal SLA (suggested: 7 days).

Enforcement Examples

  • Example: repeated off-topic posting -> private warning → 24h mute → role restriction.
  • Example: sharing private member info -> immediate removal + ban + evidence export + report.

Content Integrity & Editorial Safety

Publishing Principles

  • Attribution: cite sources where available; prefer primary sources.
  • Verification: label unverified claims clearly (use a standardized tag like [UNVERIFIED]).
  • Sensitivity: remove or redact personally identifying information unless explicit written consent exists.

Research Handling Guidelines

  1. Store sensitive materials in access-restricted channels or external secure storage.
  2. For embargoed content, use role-based gating and record access lists.
  3. When sharing scraped or aggregated material, document collection method and any limitations.

Editorial Review Workflow

Suggested lightweight workflow for research notes or short posts:

  1. Draft in a private research channel (or document repo).
  2. Peer-review by two collaborators (quick check for sourcing and sensitive content).
  3. Flag with [READY] and publish to the relevant public channel or paid newsroom area.

Paid Access (Newsroom Floor)

Paid content channels are access-controlled. Transactions and pass management must be handled via the designated payment system; treat purchase records as confidential. Do not share paid content publicly.

Channel Map (Top-level)

Main Channels

  • The Mainroom / Pub — general, calm discussions; no heated debates.
  • Rules & Guide — this guide and server rules (read-only for members).
  • Announcements — official server updates and research notes.
  • Support & Feedback — help requests and issue reporting (members use this to trigger moderation).

Newsroom (Paid Access)

  • Newspaper Pass — purchase to unlock the newsroom floor.
  • Behind the Scenes — editorial context and deeper analysis.
  • Research Libraries — curated primary sources.
  • Global Intelligence — early updates and syntheses.

Community & Contribution

  • Private Letters to the Editor — member messages to the owner/ops (managed by mods).
  • Apply to be a Moderator — link to application process (read-only for members).
  • Share Photos & Videos — opt-in sharing; do not repost externally without permission.

Places / Regional Channels

Members select a single home country role to access their regional channel. Country channels are topic-local: language stays English; regional cultural respect is required.

Security & Privacy Layer

Security measures must balance safety with member trust.

  • Prohibit scraping, automated data capture, and intelligence-gathering activities. Any suspected activity triggers an immediate Tier 3 review.
  • Preserve evidence without exposing identities publicly.
  • Use role-based access for sensitive materials; audit access quarterly.

Legal & Platform Compliance

Remind members: participation implies acceptance of these conditions. The server enforces compliance with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. The community operates under England & Wales jurisdiction for legal matters.

Mandatory Disclosures

Members must disclose if they act on behalf of a third party (employer, agency, government, or media). Undisclosed representation is prohibited and grounds for removal.

Prohibited Activities (Summary)

  • Intelligence collection, scraping, network mapping, covert monitoring.
  • Influence operations, brigading, astroturfing.
  • Recording or publishing server content externally without prior written permission.
  • Sexual or romantic approaches to staff or owners via server channels.

Operations Playbook

Onboarding Flow for New Collaborators

  1. Member reads Rules & Guide and acknowledges via reaction in onboarding message.
  2. Assign base roles (member, regional) and opt-in categories.
  3. Grant contributor roles after a probation period and verification of intent.

Moderator Responsibilities

  • Monitor assigned channels and respond within the agreed SLA.
  • Log incidents, apply tiered actions, and participate in weekly syncs.
  • Maintain neutrality; escalate policy-ambiguous cases to a panel for review.

AI Integration Layer (Optional)

AI tools can assist with summarization, citation extraction, and moderation triage. Use AI only in ways that preserve member privacy and comply with platform policies.

  • Recommended: automated triage to flag potential Tier 2/3 incidents for human review.
  • Never use AI to make final enforcement decisions—humans must review and authorize actions.
  • Record AI-assisted actions in the mod log and note the model/version used.
