What is ShiBaby™?

In Plain English: ShiBaby turns all your baby messages, photos, and notes into one smart document that updates itself.

🐴

The Horse Example

Canter™ system for stables shows how this works:

  • Staff post everything to ONE WhatsApp group
  • AI reads messages and updates the document
  • Everyone sees the same up-to-date information
  • Works offline and updates automatically
👶

The Baby Version

Exact same system, adapted for parents:

  • Family posts everything to ONE WhatsApp group
  • AI reads messages and updates baby document
  • Everyone sees same feeding, sleep, health info
  • Works offline during emergencies

Setup in 20 Minutes

You don't need to be tech-savvy! If you can send email, you can do this.

1

Get Your ShiBaby Template

Download our ready-to-use template or ask AI to create one.

// Option 1: Download template wget https://shibaby.com/template.html // Option 2: Ask AI to create one "Create a ShiBaby HTML document for a newborn named Alex. Include feeding tracker, sleep monitor, medical records, and milestone sections using Brotherly Format."
2

Create Free GitHub Account

GitHub is like Google Drive for documents that can become websites. 100% free.

1. Go to github.com in your browser 2. Click "Sign up" 3. Enter email, create password 4. Choose username (e.g., family-baby-care) 5. Verify your email
3

Upload Your Document

Turn your ShiBaby document into a live website.

1. On GitHub, click "+" → "New repository" 2. Name: baby-care (or anything) 3. Choose "Public" 4. Click "Create repository" 5. Click "Add file" → "Create new file" 6. Name: index.html (exactly!) 7. Paste your ShiBaby HTML code 8. Click "Commit new file"
4

Activate Your Free Website

One click turns your document into a live website.

1. Go to repository "Settings" 2. Click "Pages" in left menu 3. Under "Branch", select "main" 4. Click "Save" 5. Wait 1-2 minutes 6. Your site is live at: https://your-username.github.io/baby-care/

🎉 Congratulations! Your ShiBaby system is now live. Save that website link - it's your family's baby dashboard.

5

Customize for Your Baby

Edit the document to add your baby's information.

1. Go to your index.html file on GitHub 2. Click the pencil icon (✏️) to edit 3. Update the YAML baby profile: <!-- YAML Baby Profile --> <!-- --- baby_name: "Your Baby's Name" birth_date: "2025-01-15" parents: ["Parent One", "Parent Two"] pediatrician: "Dr. Name" emergency_contact: "123-456-7890" --- --> 4. Scroll down, click "Commit changes"

Daily Workflow

How your family uses ShiBaby every day

Part 1: For ALL Family Members

Create "Baby Intelligence" WhatsApp Group

The family creates a WhatsApp group (e.g., "👶 Baby Intelligence"). Add all caregivers, grandparents, nanny.

Post EVERYTHING to That Group

Rule: If it's not in the group, it didn't happen. This forces all communication into one place the AI can read.

Part 2: For Primary Parent (The AI Workflow)

How you "synthesize" all the WhatsApp data into your living document:

1

Family posts messages, photos, voice notes to WhatsApp all day

2

2-3 times daily, open WhatsApp and copy all new messages

3

Open your preferred AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.)

4

Use the Magic AI Prompt (below) to process updates

5

AI generates updated HTML for your baby document

6

Copy new HTML, paste into GitHub, click "Commit changes"

7

Within 1 minute, your baby website updates for everyone
