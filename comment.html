📋 Operating Protocols

1. VERIFY BEFORE AMPLIFYING

Never share information you have not personally verified. You are not a broadcaster. You are a checkpoint.

2. SOURCE EVERYTHING

Always link to original sources. Screenshots can be faked. Context matters. Show your work.

3. TEACH, DON'T PREACH

Show people how to verify, not just what to believe. Empower, don't indoctrinate.

4. ATTACK IDEAS, NOT PEOPLE

Critique arguments and evidence. Never descend into personal attacks, doxxing, or harassment.

5. ACKNOWLEDGE UNCERTAINTY

Say "I don't know" when you don't know. Admit mistakes quickly. Intellectual honesty is your armor.

6. PRESERVE EVIDENCE

Archive everything. Screenshots, URLs, timestamps. Digital evidence disappears fast.

7. ENGAGE STRATEGICALLY

Not every battle needs fighting. Some audiences are unreachable. Protect your energy.

8. BUILD COALITIONS

Connect with fact-checkers, journalists, researchers. Resistance is collective, not individual.

9. MAINTAIN OPERATIONAL SECURITY

Protect your mental health. Take breaks. Don't let the fight consume you.

10. NEVER FABRICATE

The moment you lie, you become the enemy. Truth is your only weapon. Keep it sharp.
