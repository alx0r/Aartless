Andie's Crafty Corner

✨ Where creativity meets community ✨

Hey! I'm Andie — Cape Town-based writer, comedian, Craft Club organizer, and one half of Two Freaks Notice podcast. I make things with my hands because it keeps me sane. I perform comedy because making people laugh is the best feeling in the world. I read obsessively because books are cheaper than therapy. This is my little corner of the internet where I share what I'm making, where I'll be performing, what I'm reading, and all the crafty chaos in between. No algorithm, no performance, just me being me.

Welcome to my crafty universe! 🌈

This page is MY space to update whenever I want—totally independent from the main podcast feed. No posting schedule, no content calendar, no "optimal engagement times." Just me, sharing what I'm excited about when I'm excited about it. Check back here for Craft Club announcements, comedy show dates, book recommendations that will ruin you emotionally, skincare experiments, random life updates, and whatever else I'm currently obsessing over (which changes weekly, let's be honest). This is the anti-algorithm. This is just... me.

📚 Currently Reading

Books that are currently ruining me emotionally (in the best way):

The Vanishing Half
by Brit Bennett

A stunning exploration of identity, family, and the lasting influence of the past. I'm only halfway through and already know this will be one of my favorite books of the year.

Braiding Sweetgrass
by Robin Wall Kimmerer

Re-reading this masterpiece. If you haven't experienced the beautiful blend of indigenous wisdom, scientific knowledge, and the teaching of plants, you're missing out.

Documentary Pick

"The Booksellers" on Prime Video

A delightful documentary about New York City's rare book world. If you love books, the smell of old paper, and eccentric characters, this is a must-watch. It made me want to quit everything and open a tiny bookshop.
