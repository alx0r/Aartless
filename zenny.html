How It Works

Your Portal Journey

1

Intention Consultation

We meet to discuss your retreat vision, participant experience, and technical needs. Share your retreat theme, schedule, and must-have features. 45-minute session via video or in-person.

2

Portal Design

We create a unique portal design tailored to your retreat energy. Choose from our three base styles (Alpine, Forest, Sacred) or request something entirely custom. Review mockups and provide feedback.

3

Content & Resources

We build your portal with all retreat details, schedules, resources, and participant information. Set up your GitHub repository, configure hosting, and prepare for launch.

4

Training & Activation

One-hour training session where we teach you how to make updates, add resources, and manage content. You'll learn simple editing techniques for real-time updates during your retreat.

5

Evolution & Integration

Your portal evolves through pre-retreat, during retreat, and post-retreat phases. Optional support packages available for major updates or adding new integration resources.
