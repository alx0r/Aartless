Democracy in the AI Era

In an age of information overload and political polarization, citizens need tools that cut through the noise. Traditional media and social platforms amplify conflict rather than clarity.

Lustra represents a new approach — using artificial intelligence to serve democracy, not disrupt it. Our algorithms are designed to find truth in complexity, not to maximize engagement through outrage.

We believe technology should empower citizens with knowledge, not manipulate them with algorithms optimized for attention. This is civic intelligence for the 21st century — transparent, accessible, and built on ethical AI principles.
