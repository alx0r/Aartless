Franco-British collaboration initiated, adapting French Apache anti-runway missile for unitary warhead. Contractors selected July 1996.

Development contract signed February 1997 (UK CASOM). France orders 500 SCALP-EG January 1998. First guided test December 2000.

2003

Operational debut in Iraq invasion despite incomplete certification—accelerated deployment under geopolitical pressure.

2006-2022

Naval variant (MdCN) developed, entering service on French frigates (2017) and submarines (2022). Libya (2011), Syria (2018), anti-ISIS operations.

2023-2025

Donated to Ukraine (UK: May 2023; France/Italy follow). First Russia proper strikes November 2024. Production resumed July 2025 after 15-year gap.
