The Ancient Lineage

Philibert Faure was born during the Renaissance, approximately 495 years ago. The Faure surname is of French origin, likely indicating a blacksmith or metalworker (from Latin "faber"). This line likely represents French Huguenot ancestry.

About This Archive

This family tree spans nearly 500 years and 14 generations, from the Renaissance-era France of Philibert Faure (ca. 1530) to the present day. The Teggin line traces English roots back to Georgian Britain (ca. 1787), while the Faure line represents French Huguenot heritage. The convergence of these two distinct European lineages through the Moller family creates your unique heritage.
