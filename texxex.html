What They're Saying

⭐ Featured Track

You Love Who You Love

The titular track features Langa Mavuso, and gives listeners a great balance of two distinct vocal styles. This is dedication to optimism without dismissing the grief—a stunningly hopeful record that sticks with you long after the final note is done.

Featuring: Langa Mavuso

The Journey

Track 01

Hey Lord

A striking vulnerability and softness in the acoustic-heavy sound. This track tells the tale of someone rediscovering themselves. A conversation with faith, raw and honest.

Track 02

This Room Feels Like Hell

The glowing part of maturation. Acoustic-driven, emotionally heavy, and deeply personal. This is where the catharsis begins.

Track 03

You Love Who You Love (feat. Langa Mavuso)

The heart of the EP. A duet that balances two distinct vocal styles, exploring acceptance and the beauty of surrendering to love without conditions.

Track 04

A Father's Prayer

Optimism meets grief. A reflection on fatherhood, faith, and the hopes we carry for the next generation. Warm, hopeful, and deeply moving.

Themes of the EP

🙏

Faith & Surrender

A journey back to self and to God. Exploring what it means to surrender to something greater.

💔

Acceptance

Reaching a point where you accept yourself, your journey, and who you love—without judgment.

🌱

Growth & Maturation

The show of maturation. Rediscovering yourself through vulnerability and honest reflection.

❤️

Love Without Conditions

You love who you love. This EP celebrates love in all its forms—messy, beautiful, and real.
