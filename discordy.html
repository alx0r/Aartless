🎯 Vision: Scenius, Not Genius

This server is Ali's digital newsroom: a calm, small "third space" where shared learning and collective thinking (what Brian Eno describes as scenius) can happen. It is also a research and writing workspace, functioning as a real-time wire of notes, sources, and ideas.

What is Scenius?

The focus is on collective creativity and community-driven knowledge over individual celebrity or parasocial worship. Scenius emphasizes the intelligence and intuition of a whole cultural scene rather than celebrating individual creators.

Watch Brian Eno explain "scenius" →

Operational Philosophy

  • Research-Focused: A working newsroom, not a fan club
  • Calm Third Space: Designed for thoughtful discourse and shared learning
  • Real-Time Wire: Functions as a living document of notes, sources, and emerging ideas
  • Community Over Celebrity: Access to Ali is not a feature; collective contribution is the value

🚀 Getting Started: Customize Your View

To reduce channel clutter and improve user flexibility, the server uses an opt-in system. Follow these steps to set up your personalized experience:

  1. Go to Channels & Roles
    Navigate to the Channels & Roles section in the Discord server
  2. Select Your Home Country
    Choose ONE country (where you currently live) to unlock access to your regional chatroom
  3. Opt Into Categories
    Select which content categories you want to see:
    • CREATOR ARMY
    • HOBBY LOBBY
    • SHARE YOUR IDEAS
  4. Specific Channel Access
    Some channels (like India) require you to react to a specific announcement message to gain access
📹 Video Tutorials Available

Short video tutorials are available in the announcement channels to guide you through these setup steps.

🧵 How to Use Threads

Threads are your primary tool for organizing deeper conversations and preventing valuable content from getting lost in busy channels.

When to Create a Thread:

  • Sharing something long, detailed, or valuable
  • Deep thoughts, research findings, or resource collections
  • Creating "rabbit holes" for unpacking complex ideas

Why Use Threads:

  • Prevents important messages from disappearing in active chat
  • Makes it easy for Ali and others to find and reference discussions later
  • Keeps conversations tidy and organized
💡 Thread Best Practice

Anyone can create threads—don't be shy! Dive in and create space for your ideas. A video demonstration is available in server announcements.

💡 Discord Mastery: Pro Tips & Features

Master these Discord features to enhance your experience in our digital newsroom and communicate more effectively.

🧵
Thread Organization

Create threads for focused discussions. Right-click any message or use the # button in channels to start organized conversations that don't clutter main channels.

🔔
Notification Control

Right-click channels to mute or customize notifications. Use @mentions sparingly and respect others' notification preferences.

📌
Pinned Messages

Check pinned messages in each channel for important resources and references. Moderators pin key information you shouldn't miss.

🔍
Powerful Search

Use Discord's search (Ctrl+K) to find specific messages, links, or files. Filter by user, channel, date, or has:link, has:file modifiers.

🎭
Custom Status

Set a custom status to share what you're researching or working on. This helps others understand your focus areas.

⚡
Quick Switcher

Press Ctrl+K to quickly jump between channels and conversations without using your mouse.

🔬 Research Workflow Tips
  • Use Threads for Deep Dives: Start a thread when sharing research findings to keep discussions organized
  • Cross-Reference with Links: Use Discord's message linking feature to connect related discussions
  • Bookmark Important Messages: Right-click messages and select "Bookmark" to save them for later reference
  • Organize with Categories: Our server uses categories to group related channels - pay attention to these groupings

Mobile Optimization

📱 Mobile App Tips
  • Enable push notifications for @mentions in important channels
  • Use the mobile app's quick reply feature for faster responses
  • Download the Discord app for better performance over browser
  • Adjust text size in settings for comfortable reading
