📋 Project Phases Overview

⚠️ CRITICAL FIRST STEP - Regulatory Compliance

Before proceeding with any research activities, ensure compliance with South African regulations (GMO Act, Nagoya Protocol, DoA/DOH/DFFE permits). Contact relevant authorities if using GM organisms or indigenous strains.

BEFORE JULY 4

Phase 1: Preparation & Sterile Setup

Lead: Team member with clinical hygiene expertise

  • Regulatory compliance check
  • Designate sterile workspace
  • Set up Still Air Box (SAB)
  • Prepare perlite for SGFC
  • Assign team roles
JULY 4 START

Phase 2: Inoculation

Duration: Single day procedure

  • Personal sterilization protocols
  • Syringe preparation & sterilization
  • PF TEK Kit inoculation
  • Labeling & dark storage setup
  • Temperature control (20-27°C)
4-6 WEEKS LATER

Phase 3: Fruiting

Trigger: 100% colonization achieved

  • Cake extraction & vermiculite removal
  • Cold shock (6-12 hours in fridge)
  • SGFC setup with prepared perlite
  • Daily misting & fanning routine
  • Harvest when veils break
POST-HARVEST

Phases 4 & 5: Processing & QC

Focus: Documentation & quality control

  • Weighing & drying procedures
  • Contamination assessment
  • Lab notebook completion
  • Data integrity (ALCOA+ principles)
  • Equipment cleaning & storage

🚨 Critical Safety Reminders

  • Never open contaminated containers - Discard immediately to prevent spore release
  • Sterile technique is paramount - One contamination can ruin entire batches
  • Cape Town rainy season considerations - Extra humidity control needed
  • Temperature maintenance - Use heating cables for 20-27°C range
  • Documentation before disposal - Record everything, even failures

📚 Documentation Standards (ALCOA+ Principles)

Remember: Attributable • Legible • Contemporaneous • Original • Accurate • Complete • Consistent • Enduring • Available

Record everything in permanent ink, never erase mistakes, include all data (even "bad" results), and maintain chronological order.
