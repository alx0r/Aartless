What Is This Discord Server?

This server is Ali's digital newsroom: a calm, small "third space" where shared learning and collective thinking (what Brian Eno describes as scenius) can happen. It is also a research and writing workspace, functioning as a real-time wire of notes, sources, and ideas.

🎯 Vision: Scenius, Not Genius

The focus is on collective creativity and community-driven knowledge over individual celebrity or parasocial worship.

Getting Started: Customize Your View

🚀 Quick Setup Guide

To reduce channel clutter and improve user flexibility, the server uses an opt-in system. Follow these steps:

  1. Go to the Channels & Roles section in Discord
  2. Select Your Home Country: Choose ONE country (where you live now) to unlock access to your regional chatroom
  3. Opt Into Categories: Choose which categories you want to see:
    • CREATOR ARMY
    • HOBBY LOBBY
    • SHARE YOUR IDEAS
  4. Specific Channel Roles: Some channels (like India) require you to react to a specific announcement message to gain access

Short video tutorials are available in the announcement channels to guide you through these steps.

How to Use Threads

🧵 Threads: Your Conversation Organizer

When to Create a Thread:

  • If you have something long, detailed, or valuable to share
  • For deep thoughts, research, or resource collections
  • To create "rabbit holes" for unpacking complex ideas

Why Use Threads:

  • Prevents important messages from getting lost in busy chat
  • Makes it easy for Ali and others to find, reference, or join discussions later
  • Keeps conversations tidy and organized

How to Make a Thread: Anyone can create threads. A short video in the server announcements demonstrates how. Don't be shy—dive in and create space for your ideas!

Core Community Rules

📜 Essential Guidelines

  • 🤝 Respect the space: This is a calm room for research, not a debate forum. No heated arguments or identity conflicts.
  • 📚 Stay on topic: Channels are for research, notes, and discussion related to the work.
  • 🔒 Protect privacy: Do not share or screenshot anything outside this server without consent.
  • 👂 Balance participation: Listen as much as you talk. Contribute with sources, context, or thoughtful feedback.
  • 🚫 No spam, cliques, or self-promo: Share projects only if directly relevant to research.
  • 🛑 Keep it safe: No hate, harassment, or disruptive behaviour.

✅ Healthy Disagreement vs. ❌ Derailment

Healthy Disagreement (Welcome) Derailment (Results in Removal)
Argue a point respectfully Reframing systemic critiques into narrow quibbles
Share evidence to support your view Using "trust me bro" instead of evidence
Push back with constructive feedback Talking down to others or being patronizing
Strengthen debate through dialogue Getting defensive or personalizing discussions
Repeat behavior that drags debates off course
