Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What level of experience is required? +
This immersion is designed for intermediate to advanced practitioners with at least 1-2 years of consistent yoga practice. You should be comfortable with basic poses, vinyasa sequencing, and holding poses for several breaths. If you're unsure, please contact us to discuss.
What's included in the price? +
Everything except transportation to/from the venue. Included: 7 nights accommodation, all meals and snacks, yoga instruction, workshops, materials, access to all facilities (pool, sauna, trails), and shuttle from designated meeting point.
What's the cancellation policy? +
Full refund minus $500 processing fee if cancelled 90+ days before start date. 50% refund if cancelled 60-89 days before. No refund within 60 days of start date. We strongly recommend travel insurance for unexpected circumstances.
Can I come solo? +
Absolutely! Many participants come solo and find it to be a profound experience. The small group size (12 max) creates intimate connections, and you'll have both communal time and private space for reflection. Solo travelers often form lasting friendships.
What about dietary restrictions? +
All meals are vegetarian. We accommodate vegan, gluten-free, and other dietary restrictions with advance notice. Our chef is experienced in creating delicious meals for various dietary needs. Please inform us of any allergies or restrictions during registration.
Is there wifi and cell service? +
Limited cell service is available. Wifi is provided in common areas for emergencies and necessary communication. We encourage a digital detox during the retreat to fully immerse in the experience, but understand if you need to stay minimally connected.
What's the weather like in March? +
March is beautiful in the mountains—days are typically 55-65°F with cool evenings around 40-45°F. Mornings can be crisp. Our studio is climate-controlled, and we practice both indoors and outdoors (weather permitting). Pack layers for temperature variations.
How do I get to the retreat center? +
We provide shuttle service from a designated meeting point (details sent upon registration). The nearest airport is 90 minutes away. Airport pickup can be arranged for an additional fee. Carpooling options often available—we'll connect registered participants.
