About Tayla

Her Story

Tayla Pringle brings a unique multicultural perspective to English education, with roots in South Africa's diverse linguistic landscape. Her background informs a teaching philosophy that celebrates linguistic diversity while mastering the nuances of English.

With a passion for poetry and Gothic literature, Tayla infuses her lessons with the rich tapestry of English literary tradition, from classic works to contemporary voices.

Educational Philosophy

Tayla believes in education that is:

  • Accessible: Removing barriers to learning through thoughtful design
  • Engaging: Connecting with students' interests and cultural contexts
  • Adaptive: Evolving with each student's unique learning journey
  • Holistic: Developing language skills within broader communicative contexts

Her approach blends traditional pedagogical excellence with innovative, student-centered methodologies.

Teaching Methodology

The Gothic & Classic Influence

Tayla's unique approach draws inspiration from Gothic literature's rich language and complex narratives, helping students appreciate the depth and versatility of English.

By studying texts from authors like the Brontë sisters, Edgar Allan Poe, and Mary Shelley, students develop an ear for sophisticated language patterns and literary devices.

This classical foundation is balanced with contemporary applications, ensuring relevance to modern communication needs.

Personalized Learning Systems

Each student receives a customized learning plan that adapts to their:

  • Current proficiency level
  • Learning style preferences
  • Specific goals and objectives
  • Cultural and linguistic background
  • Interests and motivations

Progress is continuously monitored, with adjustments made to optimize learning outcomes.
