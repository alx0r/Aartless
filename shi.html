Personality & Favorites

Shiloh is a happy baby with a wonderful smile that lights up the room. Favorite activities include tummy time (getting so strong!), listening to stories from Ouma Jen, and bath time in the evening. Shiloh loves crinkly books and soft rattles, especially ones with high-contrast patterns. The morning nap is sacred, and afternoons are best spent with some gentle playtime and lots of cuddles.

Birth Story

On April 17, 2024, Shiloh decided it was time to meet the world a little earlier than planned. At 36 weeks, Shiloh arrived at home with the help of midwife Lisa Chen. It was a beautiful, intimate birth surrounded by love.

Though born a bit early, Shiloh was strong and healthy from the very first breath. The premature arrival meant we needed to be extra attentive to developmental milestones, but Shiloh has been thriving and meeting every expectation beautifully.

From the very first moment, it was clear that this little one was going to bring so much light into our lives. Parents John and Zee, though on separate journeys now, are united in their love and care for Shiloh. The whole family—Ouma Jen, Uncle Alexander, and Aunt Lauren—rallied around to support and celebrate this precious new life.
