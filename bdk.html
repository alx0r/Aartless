Service Description

Learn the BDK Yoga Primary Series Flow in this series of 6 workshop-style classes. This martial arts based yoga practice uses slow, precise movements and transitions to build tremendous strength in the body and mobility in the joints.

From archer-style arrow shooting warriors to dancing dogs, BDK's primary series is a full body activation and a deeply meditative practice.

Build up to practicing the full sequence with minimal teacher interference and experience the beauty and imagery of the BDK system.

OSU! - A Japanese martial arts term used as a greeting or acknowledgment, expressing respect, determination, and perseverance.
