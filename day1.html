About the Immersion

This 5-day immersion offers a deep exploration of Matthew Sweeney's Vinyasa Krama system—an intelligent approach to sequencing that honors tradition while embracing innovation.

What is Vinyasa Krama?

Vinyasa Krama refers to the intelligent, step-by-step sequencing of yoga postures in a progressive manner. This methodology emphasizes:

  • Systematic progression from foundational to advanced practices
  • Intelligent preparation for peak postures
  • Therapeutic applications and individual modifications
  • Sustainable practice development over time

Immersion Focus

This immersion is designed for dedicated practitioners and teachers seeking to deepen their understanding of intelligent sequencing and practice methodology. Participants will gain practical tools for both personal practice and teaching applications.

About Matthew Sweeney

Matthew Sweeney

Internationally Recognized Yoga Teacher & Author

With over 25 years of teaching experience, Matthew Sweeney is known for his intelligent, creative and accessible approach to Vinyasa Yoga. He has developed a unique methodology that honors tradition while embracing innovation.

Matthew is the author of several influential books on yoga and has taught workshops and teacher trainings worldwide. His Vinyasa Krama system emphasizes intelligent sequencing, functional movement, and sustainable practice.

This immersion offers a rare opportunity to study directly with Matthew and experience his transformative approach to practice and teaching.
