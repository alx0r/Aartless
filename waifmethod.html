Giselle Human & The Waif Ethos

Giselle Human is a South African conceptual creative thinker, jewelry maker, designer, and writer whose multidisciplinary approach transcends traditional boundaries. With over a decade of experience spanning creative direction, copywriting, and bespoke jewelry design, Giselle has developed a unique methodology for bringing ideas to life.

Her company, Waif, began as a jewelry design brand in 2012 and has evolved into a holistic creative practice that applies the same meticulous attention to detail across disciplines—from branding and interior design to conceptual art installations.

"I don't just create objects; I create the story around them. The Waif Method™ is about honoring that entire journey—from the first spark of an idea to the final, tangible manifestation."
