About This Class

A great introduction to the traditional Ashtanga Primary Series. This guided class is taught according to the level of the group present, with modifications offered where needed. Whether you're completely new to Ashtanga or returning to your practice, you'll find a welcoming space to explore this powerful method.

The Primary Series (Yoga Chikitsa) is a therapeutic sequence designed to realign the body, purify the nervous system, and build strength, flexibility, and concentration. We move through standing postures, seated forward bends, twists, and backbends, linking each movement with breath.

Class Structure

  • Opening — Invocation & Pranayama
  • Sun Salutations — Surya Namaskar A & B
  • Standing Sequence — Foundational postures & balances
  • Seated Sequence — Forward bends, twists, hip openers
  • Finishing Sequence — Backbends, shoulderstand, headstand (optional)
  • Savasana — Rest & integration
  • Closing — Final chant & meditation

Note: The pace and depth of the class adapts to the students present. Some weeks we move through more of the sequence, other weeks we take time to break down specific postures.

About Eva Raal

Eva is an Ashtanga Yoga student and teacher based in Cape Town, South Africa. Her approach honors the traditional Ashtanga method while making the practice accessible and sustainable for modern practitioners.

With years of dedicated practice and study, Eva brings both technical precision and warmth to her teaching. She believes in meeting students exactly where they are, offering modifications and variations that honor individual bodies while maintaining the integrity of the practice.

Eva regularly travels to study with senior teachers and maintains a personal daily practice. She teaches with humility, humor, and a deep respect for the transformative power of consistent practice.
