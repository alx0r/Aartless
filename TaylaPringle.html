About Tayla

Her Story

Tayla Pringle brings a unique multicultural perspective to English education, with roots in South Africa's diverse linguistic landscape. Her background informs a teaching philosophy that celebrates linguistic diversity while mastering the nuances of English.

Tayla believes in education that is:

  • Holistic: Developing skills within broader contexts
