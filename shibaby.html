How It Works

Simple workflow for busy parents

1

Family Posts Updates

Everyone posts baby updates to your family WhatsApp/Telegram group.

"Emma just finished 4oz of formula at 2:15pm" "She's been napping for 2 hours now" "Temperature is 98.6, all normal"
2

AI Updates Document

The system reads messages and updates the ShiBaby document automatically.

// AI Prompt for ShiBaby Hello AI. You are the ShiBaby™ care coordinator. Here is the CURRENT HTML for Emma's care document: [PASTE CURRENT BABY CARE HTML HERE] Here are the NEW family messages: [PASTE ALL NEW FAMILY MESSAGES HERE] Update the feeding log, sleep tracker, and medical notes.
3

Document Updates Automatically

The living document reflects the latest baby status for everyone to see.

<section id="feeding"> <h3>Feeding Log</h3> <ul> <li>2:15 PM - 4oz formula</li> <li>10:30 AM - 5oz breast milk</li> </ul> </section>
4

Everyone Stays Informed

Parents, grandparents, and caregivers all see the same updated information.

Get Started in 5 Minutes

Set up your ShiBaby system today

1. Download Template

Start with our ready-to-use ShiBaby template.

wget https://shibaby.com/template.html

2. Customize Baby Profile

Add your baby's information to the YAML metadata.

<!-- YAML Baby Profile --> <!-- --- baby_name: "Your Baby's Name" birth_date: "2025-01-15" parents: ["Parent One", "Parent Two"] caregivers: ["Grandparent", "Nanny"] pediatrician: "Dr. Name" --- -->

3. Set Up Family Communication

Create a family WhatsApp/Telegram group for baby updates.

// Family group rules: - Everyone posts baby updates here - Include time and details - Photos welcome!

4. Start Using & Updating

Open your ShiBaby document and begin the workflow.

open baby-care-plan.html // Or host it online and share with your care team
