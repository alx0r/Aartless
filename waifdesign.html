Recent Milestones

Key achievements in the development of Alexander's SpeakHard

November 2025

First Ceramic Prototypes Completed

The Scholar's Cup has passed initial testing for weight, balance, and thermal properties. Client approved the rust and cream glaze combination that references aged books and coffee stains.

October 2025

Brand Identity Finalized

Color palette, typography, and logo concept approved. Menu copywriting in progress. Tone of voice guidelines established—intellectual yet accessible, curious but not pretentious.

September 2025

Space Secured in Cape Town CBD

120sqm heritage building with exposed brick and high ceilings. Perfect for the salon atmosphere we envisioned. Negotiated favorable lease terms with heritage preservation considerations.

Current Focus

Where our attention is directed in the immediate present

Ceramic Production Scaling

"The ceramic production is our critical path. Need to scale from prototypes to production quantities while maintaining the handmade quality. Testing different clay bodies for durability in a commercial setting."

"Each piece should feel like it has history—like it's been part of important conversations for years."

Current challenges: Achieving consistent glaze results across multiple firings while preserving the unique character of each piece.

Interior Layout Refinement

"Simultaneously working on the interior layout—how to create intimate nooks while maintaining flow. The bookshelves need to feel abundant but not overwhelming."

"The space should encourage both solitary reflection and spirited debate."

Key considerations: Lighting design to create different moods throughout the day, acoustic treatment for conversation comfort, and flexible seating arrangements.
