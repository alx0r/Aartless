Executive Summary

The Murdoch media empire, spanning News Corp and Fox Corporation, has undergone a historic leadership transition with Rupert Murdoch stepping down in 2023 and power consolidating with his eldest son, Lachlan Murdoch. This analysis examines the succession process, the $3.3 billion settlement that resolved family disputes, and the implications for the future of one of the world's most influential media conglomerates.

Fact Check: Key Events

September 21, 2023: Rupert Murdoch announces he will step down as chairman of News Corp and Fox Corporation. This is verified by multiple news sources including Reuters, CNN, and The New York Times.

December 2024: Nevada probate court rejects Murdoch's petition to amend the family trust, calling it a "carefully crafted charade."

September 8, 2025: $3.3 billion settlement announced, securing Lachlan Murdoch's control over the media empire.

The transition represents not a fundamental change in direction but a strategic consolidation and modernization of the family's conservative political project. The core "Murdoch Doctrine" — leveraging media assets for political influence and commercial advantage — remains firmly intact under Lachlan's leadership.

The Succession Process: A "Billionaire Soap Opera"

The Long War of Succession

The question of who would succeed Rupert Murdoch has been a subject of intense speculation since the 1990s. The primary contenders were Lachlan, James, and Elisabeth Murdoch, with Prudence largely remaining on the sidelines.

The brothers represented two divergent paths for the family empire:

  • Lachlan: Conservative-leaning, seen as the ideological heir aligned with Rupert's right-wing populist worldview
  • James: Increasingly liberal, resigned from News Corp board in 2020 citing editorial disagreements

Failed Legal Gambit and Settlement

Following Rupert's 2023 announcement, he and Lachlan initiated a legal maneuver to permanently secure Lachlan's control by amending the Murdoch Family Trust. This backfired when a Nevada court rejected the petition in December 2024.

The subsequent $3.3 billion settlement in September 2025 achieved their primary objective: Lachlan's absolute control. The agreement involved buying out the stakes of Prudence, Elisabeth, and James, with each receiving approximately $1.1 billion.

The Media Empire: Structure and Reach

The Murdoch empire spans three primary entities post-2013 split and 2019 Disney sale: Fox Corporation, News Corp, and private holdings. The family controls approximately 39% voting power via Class B shares, ensuring family dominance despite public ownership.

Global Influence and Infrastructure

The empire's infrastructure includes TV stations in 18 US markets (reaching 37% of households), production studios, digital platforms, printing plants in Australia/UK, and data services like Dow Jones Newswires.

This structure allows centralized control from New York with regional autonomy, enabling tailored political influence across different markets.

Civic Impact and Political Influence

The Murdoch empire's ownership induces civic change by amplifying conservative, populist narratives, polarizing discourse, and influencing elections and voter behavior. Academic studies substantiate this influence across multiple democracies.

United States

Fox News has fused with Trumpism, elevating demagogic figures and politicizing truth. Research indicates this contributed to the 2016 election outcome and the January 6 Capitol attack. Studies show ownership shifts (e.g., Wall Street Journal post-2007) lead to right-leaning content, reducing civic engagement through polarization.

United Kingdom

The Sun has influenced elections from Thatcher to Blair and played a significant role in the Brexit campaign. The tabloidization of news has eroded public trust in media institutions. Research links Murdoch endorsements to measurable voter shifts in UK elections.

Australia

With approximately 70% dominance of the print market, Murdoch outlets significantly sway elections through anti-Labor campaigns. This media concentration fosters political complacency and limits diverse viewpoints. Scholars note Murdoch's interventions often align with commercial interests.

Academic Perspective

Studies estimate Murdoch media shifts voter turnout and affiliation by 1-3% in key elections, compounding to significant policy impacts on issues like climate change and immigration.

Future Outlook Under Lachlan Murdoch

Lachlan Murdoch's leadership is expected to continue the conservative editorial direction while modernizing the business approach. Key expectations include:

  • Digital Expansion: Increased investment in streaming and digital platforms like Tubi
  • Sustained Conservatism: Continued right-leaning editorial stance across key properties
  • Potential M&A: Speculation about recombining Fox and News Corp or other strategic acquisitions
  • Externalized Opposition: James Murdoch's $1.1 billion payout could fund rival media ventures or anti-disinformation initiatives

The settlement has potentially externalized the family conflict, creating a well-funded opposition with intimate knowledge of the empire's inner workings.
