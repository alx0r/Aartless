Joy Grows When It's Shared

Collecting gifts, food, and essentials for families in need, while creating spaces for young people to share their voices, ideas, and stories.

🎁 What Is This?

A 4-week community series for young adults. We volunteer together, have real conversations, and make a real impact.

Each week features short talks from students, young professionals, and community leaders. Real stories about kindness, resilience, and showing up for each other.

📅 The Four Fridays

22 November
Planning Day
Come help plan! Shape the vision, set goals, and organize the drive together.
29 November
Sorting Day
Volunteer session! Sort and pack donations together. Music on, team vibes strong.
6 December
Delivery Day
The best part! Deliver gifts and essentials to families. See the impact of your work.
13 December
Thank You Day
Celebrate everyone! Gratitude moments, shout-outs, and shared reflections.
