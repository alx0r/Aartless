Our Story

Valley Shala emerged as a collaboration of friends and family: a little passion project that unexpectedly became a unique workshop and event venue. Our space is a true hidden gem of the Valley.

What began as a bit of an experiment has slowly developed into an eclectic but selective venue, attracting fascinating collaborators and engaging visitors in deep transformative experiences.

We aim to offer only what we truly believe in, to create a space for authentic teachers to share their knowledge and skills, and to be a world class venue specialising in yogic and therapeutic practices.

Our Yoga Practices

YogaSynergy

Experience the healing and regenerative power of the YogaSynergy practice. Founded by Australian Yoga Master and physiotherapist Simon Borg-Olivier, this practice is traditional yoga for the modern body.

Hatha Yoga

A slow (but strong) and mindful asana practice, with a focus on breath and posture alignment. Incorporating some flow and some longer holds, this traditional practice brings the mind back into the body.

Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga

This may be a class or a workshop series. One of our passionate teachers will either guide you through a regular "led class", giving you a taste of the sequence, or use a block of classes to offer you the benefits of building your self-practice.

Short Courses

Explore a new style of yoga or experience different practices and techniques in our specially designed short courses. These are groups of classes aimed at providing practitioners with tools to enhance their own self-practice.

Monthly Workshops

Join one of our experienced teachers for a deeper dive into the practice. These workshops take place monthly and are a chance to explore more advanced asana practice or other specific aspects of the practice.

Pranayama & Meditation

Look out for events, workshops and classes aimed at some of the more subtle 'limbs' of Yoga. Mantra chanting sessions, pranayama workshops and meditation classes as well as day/weekend retreats.

Our Ethos

Authenticity & Collaboration

Our "Untitled Format" is about transparency. In that spirit, our ethos is simple: we offer only what we truly believe in. We are not a high-volume studio, but a selective venue for authentic teachers to share their knowledge.

We value collaboration over competition and believe in creating a sanctuary for deep, transformative experiences. This space is built on the passion of friends and family, and we extend that welcome to every collaborator and visitor.
