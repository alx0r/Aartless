MITRE: The Cybernetic War Machine

How a "non-profit" became permanent infrastructure for state surveillance and military power

What MITRE Actually Is

Official story: A trusted, independent, non-profit research center serving the public interest in national security and civilian infrastructure.

Reality: A permanent government contractor that operates as infrastructure—too embedded to fail, too classified to scrutinize, too "non-profit" to question.

Key fact: MITRE is a Federally Funded Research and Development Center (FFRDC). This means no competitive bidding, permanent funding, and a mandate that survives every administration. They've been at it since 1958.

The Trump Connection: Continuity of Infrastructure

Trump's presidency (2017-2021, and again from 2025) reveals something crucial: MITRE doesn't care who's president.

Why Trump Makes MITRE More Visible

  • Border surveillance expansion: MITRE builds the tracking systems, biometric databases, and predictive enforcement tools ICE and CBP use
  • Cyber Command growth: Offensive cyber operations require MITRE's kill chain frameworks and adversary modeling
  • "America First" tech nationalism: Restricting Chinese 5G? MITRE does the threat assessments that justify it
  • Space Force establishment: New military branch needs new C4ISR (Command, Control, Communications, Computers, Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance)—MITRE specialty
The point: Whether Trump accelerates authoritarianism or Biden promises "norms," the same cybernetic infrastructure hums along underneath. MITRE operates at the level of permanent government—the administrative state that persists regardless of electoral politics.

Cybernetics: The War Machine's Operating System

MITRE emerged from SAGE (Semi-Automatic Ground Environment)—the first continent-scale computerized air defense system built to detect Soviet bombers. This origin story matters because it established the template for all subsequent control infrastructure.

The Cybernetic Logic

THREAT (input) ↓ SENSING (radar, cameras, data collection) ↓ PROCESSING (algorithms, threat models, AI) ↓ RESPONSE (automated countermeasures) ↓ EVALUATION (effectiveness assessment) ↓ NEW THREATS (discovered by the response) ↓ [loop continues, faster each time]

This isn't just technology—it's an epistemology. The world becomes:

  • Detectable: via sensors everywhere
  • Knowable: via processing and modeling
  • Predictable: via machine learning on behavioral data
  • Controllable: via automated feedback loops

Latour's Network: How MITRE Stabilized

Bruno Latour shows us that power comes from enrolling diverse actors into stable networks. MITRE is a master class in this.

The Enrolled Actors

  • DoD/Intelligence: "We need technical solutions to security threats"
  • Universities: "We need funding and prestige research"
  • Corporations: "We need early access to government requirements"
  • Congress: "We need accountability without bureaucracy"
  • The Public: "We need safe skies and secure infrastructure"

Each thinks they're getting what they want. But MITRE occupies the obligatory passage point—you cannot route around them once systems depend on:

  • CVE (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures) database
  • ATT&CK framework (cyber threat intelligence)
  • Air traffic control standards
  • System engineering methodologies
Black-boxing: These systems become so naturalized, so "just how things work," that questioning them seems absurd. Try imagining cybersecurity without CVE IDs—you can't, because MITRE built the ontology.

Dual-Use: War Logic in Civilian Clothes

The same systems that track missiles track migrants. The same algorithms that target combatants target content moderation. This isn't accidental—it's infrastructural.

Examples of Cybernetic Colonization

  • Air traffic control: Built from bomber tracking—same sensors, same prediction models, same command-and-control logic
  • Border surveillance: Adapted from battlefield awareness systems—predict crossings, preempt movement, optimize interdiction
  • Critical infrastructure protection: Originally nuclear facility security—now manages power grids, water systems, with military response protocols
  • Cyber threat intelligence: NSA tradecraft packaged for corporate security—same attribution methods, same offensive/defensive ambiguity
There is no "civilian" cybernetics. Systems designed for war carry war logic into everyday life. Efficiency becomes kill-chain speed. Optimization becomes casualty minimization. Safety becomes threat elimination.

The Permanent Emergency

MITRE began tracking Soviet bombers (1958). Now it's:

  • Cyber threats
  • Bioterrorism
  • AI safety
  • Climate security
  • Quantum computing threats
  • Synthetic biology risks

The threat morphs, but the infrastructure persists. This is Giorgio Agamben's state of exception made permanent—the emergency that justifies extraordinary measures becomes the normal operating condition.

Autopoiesis: The system reproduces its own necessity. Problems require MITRE expertise → MITRE defines problems → Solutions require MITRE → Implementation reveals new problems → Loop continues.

Knowledge Enclosure: Your Taxes, Their IP

Publicly funded research becomes:

  • Classified: Removed from public scrutiny
  • Patented: Privatized as intellectual property
  • Operationalized: Deployed before democratic deliberation
  • Normalized: Becomes "just how things work"

You pay for the innovation. Then you pay again (in surveillance, in compliance costs, in loss of autonomy) when it's deployed against you.

What This Means

Technical Decisions Are Political Decisions

When MITRE defines:

  • What counts as a vulnerability (CVE)
  • How to model adversaries (ATT&CK)
  • What constitutes a threat (intelligence assessments)
  • How systems should be architected (engineering standards)

...these aren't neutral technical choices. They're political decisions about whose security matters, what futures are possible, and how power operates.

But they're made by engineers with security clearances, not through democratic process. Politics is evacuated from the very infrastructure of social life.

The Cybernetic Subject

We become subjects who are:

  • Always legible (to sensors)
  • Always predictable (to models)
  • Always modulatable (by infrastructure)
  • Never outside the feedback loop

This is war mechanized as everyday life—not occupation, but optimization; not repression, but continuous adjustment.

The Stakes

MITRE isn't the problem—it's the perfected form of what happens when:

  • Military funding shapes civilian infrastructure
  • Classification prevents democratic oversight
  • Technical systems encode political choices
  • Permanent emergency justifies extraordinary power
  • "Public interest" masks state and corporate interests

The urgent question: Can we even think outside the cybernetic closure their systems have constructed around us?

Or has the war machine become the territory itself?
