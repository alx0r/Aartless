Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code? +
Black tie optional. We want you to feel elegant and comfortable. The ceremony will be outdoors on grass, so stiletto heels may not be practical. Consider wedges or block heels for the ladies.
Can I bring children? +
We love your little ones, but we've decided to make this an adults-only celebration. We hope this gives you a chance to relax and enjoy the evening. Babysitting services can be arranged through the hotels if needed.
Will transportation be provided? +
Yes! Shuttle buses will depart from both hotel blocks at 4:00 PM on Saturday. Return shuttles will run at 11:30 PM and 1:00 AM. If you're staying elsewhere, please arrange private transportation to Villa Aurelia.
What language will the ceremony be in? +
The ceremony will be conducted in English with some Italian traditions woven throughout. Programs will be provided in both languages.
Are there dietary restrictions you can accommodate? +
Absolutely. Please indicate any dietary restrictions or allergies in your RSVP. Our caterers can accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and other dietary needs.
What's the weather like in Rome in June? +
June is beautiful in Rome, with temperatures typically ranging from 65-85°F (18-29°C). Evenings can be cooler, so consider bringing a light wrap or pashmina. The venue has both covered and open-air spaces.
Where are you registered? +
Your presence is the greatest gift. If you wish to honor us with a gift, we've created a honeymoon fund to support our travels through Italy after the wedding. Details will be shared closer to the date.
Can I take photos during the ceremony? +
We're having an unplugged ceremony—no phones or cameras during the vows, please. Our professional photographers will capture everything. After the ceremony, feel free to take all the photos you'd like and share them using #ChenThornton2026
