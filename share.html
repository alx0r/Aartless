AI: TOOL, NOT ORACLE

Artificial intelligence is being weaponized against truth. Use it correctly or don't use it at all.

Set Wikipedia as your homepage. Search information there FIRST. Use it as your primary knowledge base.
Never trust AI for facts. Use AI for processing, analysis, summarization - not truth.
Multi-AI verification: Use Claude, GPT, and other models to fact-check each other.
Learn prompt engineering for verification: "Find contradictions in this claim" / "What sources contradict this?"
Use AI to process large documents quickly, then verify claims manually with primary sources.
Resist AI autopilot: Google's AI summaries, ChatGPT search - these are truth-laundering systems.
Specific AI tools for specific tasks: Use Claude for analysis, not generation. Use GPT for brainstorming, not facts.
