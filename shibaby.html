👶 One Baby, One Document

ShiBaby™ creates a single source of truth for your baby's care: feeding schedules, sleep patterns, medical records, and developmental milestones—all in one beautiful, always-updated document.

// Your baby's entire care plan in one file <!DOCTYPE html> <html> <head> <!-- YAML Baby Metadata --> <title>Baby Emma's Care Plan</title> <style>/* All baby-friendly styles */</style> </head> <body> <!-- All baby care content --> <script>/* All functionality */</script> </body> </html>

Each ShiBaby document contains:

  • Feeding schedules and amounts
  • Sleep patterns and nap tracking
  • Diaper change logs
  • Medical records and vaccination schedules
  • Developmental milestones
  • Emergency contact information
