🔄 How It Works (No Tech Skills Needed)

We've made team management as simple as sending a WhatsApp message. Here's how it works:

1

You Communicate Normally

Keep using WhatsApp, email, or whatever you already use for team communication. Send team updates, match results, player availability, training notes - anything you normally share.

"Match tomorrow vs Pirates Youth at 3pm. Players arrive by 2pm. Sipho is injured, Thandi is available again."
2

AI Organizes Everything

Our system automatically reads your messages and updates the team document. It understands player status, match details, training schedules, and organizes everything neatly.

AI detects:
• Next match: Pirates Youth (Nov 2, 3pm)
• Player status: Sipho → injured, Thandi → available
• Updates team document automatically
3

Get Your Updated Document

You receive a simple file that contains your entire team management system - updated with the latest information. Open it anytime, anywhere, even without internet.

File: Bafana-U15-Team.html (45KB)
Contains: Squad, schedule, match details, player stats, training plans
4

Share With Your Team

Send the file to assistant coaches, team managers, or players' parents. They can open it on any device, see the latest updates, and everything stays synchronized.

"Here's our team file with this week's schedule and lineup for Saturday's match."
