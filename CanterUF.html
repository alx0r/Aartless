System Overview

Canter™ is a dynamic stable intelligence system designed to transform fragmented daily operations into cohesive, actionable intelligence. By integrating multiple data sources—from whiteboard photos to WhatsApp chats—into a unified semantic framework, Canter™ creates living documents that evolve with your stable.

Core Innovation: Canter™ doesn't just store information—it understands relationships between horses, staff, schedules, and health data, creating a holistic view of stable operations that improves decision-making and horse welfare.

Key Features

📸 Multi-Source Data

Captures information from whiteboards, messages, forms, and veterinary systems automatically.

🧠 AI Intelligence

Understands context and relationships, turning raw data into actionable insights.

📱 Mobile-First

Designed for barn staff using phones and tablets in real-world conditions.

🔄 Living Documents

Reports that update in real-time and maintain complete history.

📱 Basic Guide for New Users

This guide is for people who are not tech experts. We'll walk you through everything step-by-step with pictures and simple language.

What is Canter™ in Simple Terms?

Think of Canter™ as a smart notebook for your stable. Instead of writing on whiteboards or sending scattered WhatsApp messages, everything goes into one place where it's organized automatically.

1

Getting Started: Opening Canter™

On your phone:

  1. Open your web browser (Safari, Chrome, or whatever you use for internet)
  2. Type in the address: canter.valleyheights.com
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen" so you have an icon like any app
  4. Log in with the username and password your manager gave you

Tip: Save your password in your phone so you don't have to type it every time!

2

Daily Task: Morning Checks

What you do:

  1. Open Canter™ on your phone
  2. Tap the big "Morning Check" button
  3. You'll see a list of all horses
  4. For each horse, tap their name and answer simple questions:
    • Is the horse eating normally? (Yes/No)
    • Any injuries or concerns? (Type a short note)
    • Water bucket full? (Yes/No)
  5. Take a photo of anything unusual (swelling, cuts, etc.)
  6. Tap "Submit" when done
Important: If something is seriously wrong (horse down, bad colic, etc.), CALL your manager immediately. Don't just put it in the app!
3

Taking Photos of Whiteboards

The easiest way to update:

  1. After you write on the whiteboard (like you always do), tap the camera button in Canter™
  2. Take a clear photo of the whiteboard (make sure it's not blurry)
  3. Tap "Upload"
  4. That's it! The system reads the whiteboard automatically

Tip: Take photos in good light. Early morning photos in dim light don't work as well.

4

Quick Updates (Like WhatsApp)

For fast messages:

  1. Tap the "Quick Update" button
  2. Select which horse it's about
  3. Type your message (just like texting):
    • "Eclipse is limping a bit on the left front"
    • "Thunder didn't finish his feed"
    • "Midnight looks great today"
  4. Hit send – your manager gets notified automatically
5

Viewing the Daily Report

See what everyone else has reported:

  1. Tap "Today's Report" on the home screen
  2. You'll see:
    • All horses and their status
    • Who's training today and when
    • Any health concerns
    • Notes from vets or farriers
  3. Everything updates live as people add information

Common Questions

Q: What if I make a mistake?

A: Don't worry! Everything can be edited. Tap the entry, select "Edit," fix it, and save. Your manager can also fix anything.

Q: What if my phone dies or has no signal?

A: Canter™ works offline! Type your updates, and they'll upload automatically when you get signal again. Use the old whiteboard as backup if needed.

Q: Do I have to use my own phone data?

A: No! Use the stable's WiFi. The system uses very little data anyway (like sending a few text messages).

Q: What if I don't know how to type something?

A: Use the voice button (microphone icon) and just speak your update. It types for you!

Q: Can I see old reports?

A: Yes! Tap "History" and pick any date. You can see everything that happened on that day.

Remember: You're not expected to be a computer expert. If something doesn't work or you're confused, ask your manager or a co-worker. Everyone is learning together!

Emergency Contact

If the system isn't working or you need urgent help:

  • System issues: Call stable office
  • Horse emergency: Call vet / manager (don't wait for the app!)
  • Technical help: Text the tech support number your manager gave you

System Architecture

Canter™ is built on a five-layer architecture that ensures robustness, flexibility, and intelligence.

Layer Function Components
L1: Data Collection Aggregate from multiple sources Whiteboard OCR, WhatsApp parsing, Mobile forms, Vet API
L2: Semantic Processing Understand and structure data NLP engine, Entity recognition, Relationship mapping
L3: Intelligence Engine Transform data into insights Pattern recognition, Trend analysis, Alert generation
L4: Presentation Layer Deliver insights Web interface, Mobile apps, Export tools, Reports
L5: Version Control Track history Git-based versioning, Audit trails, Change attribution

Daily Workflow Integration

Canter™ seamlessly integrates into your existing stable routines without disrupting workflow.

1

Morning Checks & Whiteboards

Staff complete morning checks and update physical whiteboards as usual. Photos are automatically captured and processed.

2

Messaging Integration

WhatsApp messages between staff are parsed for relevant updates (health concerns, schedule changes, farrier notes).

3

Structured Data Entry

Trainers and vets input structured data through simple mobile forms for medications, training notes, and health updates.

4

AI Processing & Synthesis

All data sources are processed, validated, and synthesized into the daily intelligence report.

5

Review & Distribution

The completed daily report is reviewed by management and distributed to relevant stakeholders.

Reflexive Statement

On Design Philosophy

Canter™ is designed with transparency and continuous improvement at its core. This documentation itself is a living document that evolves with the system.

We believe stable management systems should augment human expertise, not replace it. Canter™ is designed to handle the routine data processing so staff can focus on what matters most—horse care and training.

Known Limitations

  • Initial setup requires cultural adaptation to digital processes
  • AI processing accuracy improves with data volume over time
  • Integration with legacy systems may require custom development
  • Rural locations with poor connectivity need offline capabilities
