Recent Milestones

November 2025

First ceramic prototypes completed - The Scholar's Cup has passed initial testing for weight, balance, and thermal properties. Client approved the rust and cream glaze combination.

October 2025

Brand identity finalized - Color palette, typography, and logo concept approved. Menu copywriting in progress. Tone of voice guidelines established.

September 2025

Space secured in Cape Town CBD - 120sqm heritage building with exposed brick and high ceilings. Perfect for the salon atmosphere we envisioned.

📝 Current Focus (November 2025)

"The ceramic production is our critical path. Need to scale from prototypes to production quantities while maintaining the handmade quality. Testing different clay bodies for durability in a commercial setting."

"Simultaneously working on the interior layout - how to create intimate nooks while maintaining flow. The bookshelves need to feel abundant but not overwhelming."

What's Next

The immediate future of Alexander's SpeakHard

🎯 Q1 2026

Ceramic Production Scaling - Ramp up to produce 200+ pieces for opening inventory. Develop consistent glazing process while maintaining unique character.

🏗️ Q2 2026

Interior Build-Out - Construction begins. Custom shelving, counter installation, lighting design implementation. All materials sourced and confirmed.

📚 Q3 2026

Book Curation & Staff Training - Finalize the reading selection. Hire and train baristas who understand the intellectual salon concept.

🎪 Q4 2026

Soft Opening & Programming - Limited opening with curated guest list. Begin evening salon events, author readings, and philosophy discussions.
