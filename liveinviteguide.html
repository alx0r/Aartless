❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI data extraction? +
Our AI achieves 95%+ accuracy for structured data like dates, times, and locations. For complex context and relationships, we provide easy editing tools to refine the output. The system learns from corrections and improves over time.
What file types can I upload for processing? +
We support: PDF, DOC/DOCX, TXT, JPG/PNG (for text extraction), and direct text input from websites, emails, and chat applications. For images, we use advanced OCR to extract text from screenshots and photos of documents.
Can I use this for corporate events with sensitive information? +
Yes! We offer enterprise-grade security options including private hosting, data encryption, and compliance with GDPR/CCPA. All AI processing can be done on-premises for maximum security when required.
How does the AI handle different languages and cultures? +
Our AI supports multiple languages and understands cultural context for events. It adapts templates, formatting, and content style based on the detected language and cultural cues in your source material.
What happens if the AI makes a mistake in extraction? +
You can easily correct any mistakes during the review stage. The system learns from corrections and applies them to future processing. We also provide manual override options for complete control.
Can I integrate with my existing event management tools? +
Absolutely! We provide API access and pre-built integrations for popular event platforms, CRM systems, payment processors, and communication tools. Custom integrations can be developed for specific needs.
