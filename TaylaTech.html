Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to change how I communicate with Tayla?
No! Keep using WhatsApp, voice notes, email - whatever works for you. The system works with your existing communication style.
How much technical knowledge do I need?
None at all. If you can copy-paste text, you can use Tayla Tutor Tech. The setup takes 20 minutes with guided assistance.
What happens to my data?
Your data stays in a single HTML file that you control. You can host it anywhere or keep it locally. No cloud storage, no corporate access.
Can I customize what I track?
Absolutely! Every dashboard is completely bespoke. Track vocabulary, grammar, test scores, speaking fluency - whatever matters to your goals.
What if I stop tutoring with Tayla?
You keep your dashboard forever. It becomes a permanent record of your progress that you can continue to update or reference anytime.
