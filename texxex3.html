Festival Survival Guide

🎒 What to Pack

  • Festival ticket & ID
  • Sun hat & sunscreen
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Portable phone charger
  • Ear protection
  • Warm layers for evening
  • Cash & card
  • Wet wipes & hand sanitizer

🚗 Getting There

  • Shuttle buses from Cape Town CBD
  • Designated driver program available
  • Car pooling encouraged
  • Parking opens at 8 AM daily
  • Ride share drop-off zone at main entrance

🏥 Health & Safety

  • First aid stations at all stage areas
  • Free water refill stations
  • Safe spaces available
  • Lost & found at main info booth
  • Security contact: 076 123 4567
  • Emergency meeting points marked on map

💡 Pro Tips from Texx

  • Arrive early for smaller acts - discover new favorites!
  • Download the schedule to your phone for offline access
  • The acoustic sets at sunrise are magical
  • Food trucks near entrance have shortest lines
  • Charge devices during daytime performances
  • Meet at the giant daisy if separated from friends
