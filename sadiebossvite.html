Questions

What Brides Ask

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. We handle all the technical setup and teach you simple editing through a visual interface. If you can edit a Google Doc, you can update your invitation.

What if I want to change something after launch?

That's the whole point! Update venue details, add new photos, modify the schedule—anytime you want. Changes go live instantly. No need to contact us unless you want design changes.

How long does the invitation last?

Forever. You own the file and it's hosted on GitHub for free, permanently. Keep it as a digital keepsake, turn it into your wedding website archive, or download it anytime.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes! We can connect it to yourdomain.com or younames.wedding. Domain registration is separate (~$12/year), but we handle all the technical setup.

What happens after the wedding?

Your invitation becomes a beautiful digital memory. Add wedding day photos, thank you messages, or links to your professional photos. Many couples transform it into a permanent wedding archive.

Is this just for tech-savvy couples?

No! We've designed this for everyone. Our training makes it incredibly simple. Think of it like having a custom website that you can edit as easily as updating your Instagram bio.
