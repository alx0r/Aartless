About

The short version

Ten-plus years across integrated digital strategy, content, paid media and analytics — agency time at VML, Wunderman Thompson/Mirum and Lima Bean, working accounts from Absolut and Jameson to Capitec and Old Mutual. The kind of experience that teaches you "integrated" usually means three departments not talking to each other, and that the job is mostly translation.

In 2021 I left client work for two years to do a research Master's in Media Studies at the University of Amsterdam — a programme ranked among the world's best for it — because I wanted to understand why platforms behave the way they do, not just how to buy media on them. Spent the time on OSINT, digital propaganda and platform algorithms instead of another deck template.

Now back in Cape Town, consulting through Wolf & Lily Agency, and spending more time than is strictly billable on Generative Engine Optimisation — how brands get surfaced, summarised and occasionally misrepresented by AI systems instead of ranked on a results page. This site is partly a portfolio, partly a live test of that.

A note on how this is built

This page runs on Forever Format — a single-file HTML convention that treats a personal document the way an AI search system increasingly treats the web: something to be read, structured and cited, not just looked at. Semantic markup, embedded JSON-LD, a version number, a licence, and — because old habits from a media-theory degree die hard — a BibTeX entry, in case you ever need to cite a CV.

No build step, no tracking scripts, no external requests. What you're reading is the whole thing.
