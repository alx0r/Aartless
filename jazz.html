Signature Cocktails & Mocktails

Classic 1920s Cocktails (Choose 2-3 to focus on):

Large Batch Options & Pro Tips:

  • Punch Bowl Recipes - Make 2-3 big batches, less work during party.
  • Sangria with a Twist - White wine, gin, fruits, soda water.
  • Spiked Lemonade - Vodka, fresh lemonade, mint.
  • Pro Tip for Cocktails: Pre-batch base ingredients (spirits, juices, syrups) for your chosen signature cocktails. Store them in jugs or dispensers. This makes serving much faster – just add ice and any bubbly toppers (like soda or prosecco) per drink when serving.

Key Drink & Food Considerations

Ice is Key! You'll need a significant amount of ice, especially for cocktails for 25-30 people. Plan to buy several large bags from a supermarket or ice supplier. Assign an "Ice Warden" to monitor and replenish throughout the party.

Hydration Station: Set up an easily accessible and appealing water station. A large dispenser with sliced lemon, cucumber, or mint makes it inviting and encourages guests to stay hydrated.

Food Safety Note: For perishable finger foods (like shrimp cocktail, charcuterie boards, items with dairy), be mindful of how long they are left at room temperature, even in June. It's best to put out smaller batches and replenish them as needed to keep everything fresh and safe.

Success Tips for One-Week Planning

  • Divide and conquer - Each housemate takes specific tasks (decorations, food, music, neighbor relations). Ensure roles are crystal clear and each person knows their specific deliverables and deadlines.
  • Keep expectations realistic - Focus on atmosphere over perfection with short timeline.
  • Use the housewarming angle - People love seeing new places, gives natural conversation starters.
  • Prep what you can early - Decorations up by Thursday, food prep Thursday night.
  • Have a backup indoor plan - June weather in Cape Town can be unpredictable.
  • Document everything - This is Anna's 21st AND your first party in the new house!
  • Anna's Enjoyment: Ensure Anna gets to fully enjoy her 21st birthday celebration without being bogged down by hosting duties all night. Delegate tasks effectively so she can relax and celebrate with her guests!
