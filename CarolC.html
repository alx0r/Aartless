Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need technical skills to use this system?
No technical skills required. If you can copy-paste text from emails or conversations, you can update your intelligence hub. Setup takes 20 minutes with guided assistance.
How does this work with my current consulting practice?
It enhances your current workflow without changing it. You continue working with clients as usual, and the system automatically organizes all the information into actionable intelligence.
Can I customize dashboards for different types of clients?
Absolutely! Each client gets a completely bespoke dashboard. Family relocations, school consultations, tutor development - each has tailored tracking and resources.
What about data privacy with international clients?
Your data stays in a single HTML file that you control. No cloud storage, no corporate access. Perfect for handling sensitive international student information.
Can I use this to train other educational consultants?
Yes! The system is perfect for building consultant networks with shared resources, standardized processes, and individual client management.
