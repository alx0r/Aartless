About Alicja Pawlowska

Alicja Pawlowska is a seasoned open-source intelligence (OSINT) and cybersecurity specialist with a strong focus on threat intelligence and geopolitical analysis. With a background spanning both corporate and non-profit sectors, she brings a wealth of practical experience to her insights and analysis.

Currently serving as an OSINT Analyst at S-RM, Alicja's expertise is honed through her work in real-world scenarios, providing actionable intelligence to diverse audiences. Through this newsletter, she aims to demystify the complexities of OSINT and cybersecurity, offering readers practical knowledge and strategic perspectives to navigate the modern information landscape. Join her to stay informed on the cutting edge of digital intelligence and enhance your understanding of today's evolving threats.
