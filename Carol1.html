📋 Project Overview

What We're Building

We're creating a complete digital presence for Carol Crous, transforming 15+ years of educational consulting expertise into a scalable online business. This includes professional websites, social media accounts, content strategy, and digital products that will help international families navigate complex educational systems worldwide.

Why This Project Matters

🌍 Global Reach

Carol's expertise spans 6 countries and multiple education systems. Digital presence allows her to help families anywhere in the world, not just those who can meet in person.

📈 Scalable Income

Moving from 1-on-1 consulting to digital products, online courses, and group workshops means Carol can help more families while building sustainable income for later in life.

🎯 Targeted Marketing

International families actively search online for education consultants. A strong digital presence ensures they find Carol when they need help most.

Primary Target Markets

  • 🇪🇺
    Western European Expatriate Families
    Families relocating within Europe or to Turkey, Netherlands, UK who need guidance on international school systems and transitions.
  • 🇿🇦
    South African Families
    Families seeking international education opportunities, leveraging Carol's deep understanding of both SA and international systems.
  • 🏢
    Corporate Relocating Families
    International company employees moving with children who need expert guidance on school placement and educational transitions.
  • 🧠
    Parents of Children with Learning Differences
    Families seeking specialized support for children with learning difficulties, giftedness, or unique educational needs.

⚙️ Technical Implementation Plan

This section explains exactly what we'll build and how each piece works together. Think of this as the technical blueprint that transforms Carol's expertise into a digital business.

Phase 1: Digital Foundation (Weeks 1-2)

Goal: Establish Carol's basic digital identity across all major platforms

Step 1: Secure Digital Assets

Business Email Setup

  • Purchase domain (e.g., carolcrousconsulting.com)
  • Set up professional email: carol@[chosen domain]
  • Configure email forwarding to current email
  • Set up professional email signature with contact info

Social Media Account Creation

  • Instagram: @carolcrousconsulting (or chosen variation)
  • LinkedIn: Carol Crous - Educational Consultant
  • Facebook: Carol Crous Educational Consulting (Business Page)
  • TikTok: @carollearning (shorter handle works better)
Important: We'll create all accounts with the same name/handle variations to maintain consistency. Some names might not be available, so we'll have backup options ready.
Step 2: Professional Branding

Photo Requirements

  • Professional headshot (main profile photo across all platforms)
  • Casual working photos (for website and social content)
  • Photos with books/educational materials (builds credibility)
  • Photos in different settings (office, with clients, presenting)

Bio Development

We'll create platform-specific bios that highlight Carol's expertise:

  • LinkedIn: Professional, comprehensive (longer format)
  • Instagram: Engaging, personal (150 characters)
  • Facebook: Informative, service-focused
  • TikTok: Fun, approachable (brief)
Step 3: Supporting Platforms

Google Business Profile

  • Create business listing for local Istanbul searches
  • Add service areas (can serve clients globally)
  • Include professional photos and service descriptions
  • Set up review collection system

Etsy Shop Setup

  • Create shop for digital products and guides
  • Design shop banner and profile
  • Set up payment processing
  • Create initial product listings (guides, checklists)

Phase 2: Content Hub Development (Weeks 3-4)

Goal: Create a professional website that serves as the central hub for all Carol's services and content

Website Development

Technical Approach

  • Start Simple: Single HTML page (like this one) hosted on GitHub Pages
  • Mobile-First Design: Most users will access via phone/tablet
  • Fast Loading: Optimized for international users with varying internet speeds
  • SEO Optimized: Built to rank well in search engines

Website Sections

  • Hero Section: Clear value proposition and call-to-action
  • About Carol: Professional background and expertise
  • Services: Detailed description of consulting offerings
  • Success Stories: Client testimonials and case studies
  • Resources: Free guides and educational content
  • Contact Form: Easy way for potential clients to reach out
  • Blog Section: Educational articles and insights
Step 2: Content Creation Tools

Essential Software Stack

  • Canva Pro: For creating professional graphics, social media posts, and marketing materials
  • Google Workspace: For document creation, storage, and collaboration
  • Loom/Canva Video: For creating simple educational videos
  • Calendly/Acuity: For scheduling consultations and appointments
  • Mailchimp/ConvertKit: For email marketing and newsletter management

Content Templates to Create

  • Social media post templates (consistent branding)
  • Email newsletter template
  • Client intake form template
  • Consultation preparation guides
  • Educational resource templates

Phase 3: Content Strategy & Launch (Weeks 5-8)

Goal: Develop and implement a sustainable content strategy that positions Carol as an authority in international education consulting

Content Calendar Development

Weekly Content Rhythm

  • Monday: Educational tip or insight (all platforms)
  • Tuesday: Behind-the-scenes content (Instagram/TikTok)
  • Wednesday: Longer educational piece (LinkedIn/Blog)
  • Thursday: Client success story or testimonial
  • Friday: Resource highlight or free guide
  • Weekend: Engagement questions or community interaction

Content Batch Creation

We'll create content in batches to maintain consistency:

  • Monthly content planning session
  • Weekly content creation blocks (2-3 hours)
  • Daily engagement (30 minutes)
  • Monthly analytics review
Digital Product Development

Initial Product Offerings

  • Relocation Guide: Comprehensive guide for families moving internationally
  • School Selection Checklist: Step-by-step process for choosing the right school
  • Learning Assessment Toolkit: Tools for identifying learning styles and needs
  • IB Program Overview: Guide to International Baccalaureate programs

Pricing Strategy

  • Free resources (lead magnets)
  • Low-cost guides ($10-25)
  • Premium packages ($50-100)
  • Custom consultation packages ($200+)
