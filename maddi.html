⚡ Immediate Wins - Start Today

🎬 Content Repurposing Strategy

The Gold Mine You Already Have: You likely have tons of old content - videos, selfies, photos from river cleanups, creative moments. Let's turn these into consistent content.

Simple Formula: Good quality video clip + simple voiceover = engaging content. Start with 15-30 second clips, add your voice explaining what's happening, your thoughts, or the story behind the moment.

ADHD-Friendly Tip: Batch record voiceovers when you're feeling chatty - do 5-10 at once, then you have content ready for weeks.

📸Your Face = 7x Stronger Engagement

The Reality: Content featuring you personally performs significantly better on social media. Your authentic presence is your superpower.

Easy Implementation:

  • More selfie-style videos while doing activities
  • Direct-to-camera moments sharing thoughts
  • Include yourself in eco-warrior documentation
  • Show your face while creating music or poetry

Mindset Shift: This isn't vanity - it's connection. People follow people, not just content.

🎭 Building Your Personal Narrative

🔴 TikTok Live Strategy - First 10 Hours

Goal: Build organic audience through authentic, low-pressure live streaming.

Super Simple Approach:

  • "Study With Me" Vibes: Just casually chat while browsing, reading, or working
  • Creative Sessions: Go live while writing poetry or working on music
  • "Life Admin" Streams: Organizing, planning eco-warrior activities, general life stuff
