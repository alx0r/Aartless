Operational Summary

  1. Information wars rely on speed. Resist with stillness + proof.
  2. Propaganda thrives on repetition. Break loops, not heads.
  3. Truth moves slower, but it leaves footprints — collect them.

Forensic Steps

  • Capture evidence raw (full-screen, with clock, UTC).
  • Hash immediately (SHA256). Never rename originals.
  • Archive page + upload file to distributed storage.
  • Record provenance: who posted first, when, and where.
  • Cross-compare phrasing; look for mirrored syntax.

Tools of Verification

  • Image: InVID, TinEye, ExifTool
  • Video: ffmpeg + frame time extraction
  • Network: account creation dates, follower graphs
  • Archive: Wayback, Archive.today, IPFS pin
  • Crypto-sign: PGP, git-commit hashes, timestamped proofs

Counter-Measures

  • Post slower than they post — speed is their weapon, accuracy is yours.
  • Never amplify unverified claims (even to “debunk”).
  • Store proofs locally + on-chain; redundancy is defense.
  • Write concise, source-linked summaries — clarity outruns outrage.
  • Teach others verification. Decentralize cognition.
