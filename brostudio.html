Why We Founded Untitled Format

We created Untitled Format because we believe document infrastructure should be public, permanent, and owned by everyone—like HTML itself. Traditional document systems lock your data away in proprietary silos. We wanted something different.

Untitled Format is free and open source. Anyone can use it. Anyone can build on it. We released it to the world because knowledge infrastructure should belong to everyone.

Brotherly Protocol™ is our implementation. It's our production-proven adaptation, refined through real-world projects. When you work with Brother's Studio, you benefit from our deep knowledge as the protocol's founders—and you get an architecture that's battle-tested and reliable.

📄 Single-File Architecture

Everything in one HTML file (20-100KB). No dependencies. Works anywhere, forever.

🤖 AI-Ready Structure

Semantic HTML5 with YAML metadata. AI can read, understand, and update intelligently.

🔒 Digital Sovereignty

You own the file completely. No cloud dependencies. No subscriptions. Permanent control.

⚡ Offline-First

Loads in milliseconds. Works completely offline. Minimal bandwidth. Universal access.
