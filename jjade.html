A Strategy Grounded in Research, Not Just Trends

What's so compelling about your content is that you've intuitively avoided the major pitfalls of typical online recovery narratives. Much of the academic research into eating disorder (ED) content online highlights the dangers of what's called **"performative recovery"**—content that focuses so much on the *story* of being sick or perfectly recovered that it can inadvertently become competitive or triggering.

The Research-Backed Advantage of Your Approach: Studies show that content promoting intrinsic motivation (like curiosity, cultural learning, personal enjoyment) over extrinsic validation (likes, follower counts, praise for being "good" at recovery) leads to more sustainable well-being. Your focus on Lithuanian and Swiss culture, family, and daily life does exactly this. It frames food as part of a rich, full life—not the sole focus of it. This is a therapeutically sound approach that many creators miss.

A Potential Roadmap: From Personal Journey to Broader Impact

My goal is to support you wherever you are on your journey. To help structure our potential collaboration, here’s a possible roadmap. Think of these as phases we can explore at your own pace, or completely disregard if they don't align with your vision.

Phase 1: Fortifying Your Foundation (Here & Now)

This is about ensuring your current process is safe, sustainable, and joyful for you.

  • Content Safety Frameworks: Developing simple, non-intrusive checklists to ensure your posts are supportive for your audience and, most importantly, for you.
  • Community Cultivation: Creating moderation strategies and community guidelines that foster a truly safe space, protecting you from harmful engagement.
  • Algorithmic Strategy without Compromise: Understanding how to work *with* platform algorithms (like on Instagram and TikTok) without ever sacrificing your authenticity.

Phase 2: Defining the "Post-Recovery" Horizon (The Future)

A key question for many in recovery is, "What comes next?" Your identity is not your recovery. We can strategize how your content evolves as you do, ensuring it remains a source of joy, not an obligation.

  • Transitioning to Lifestyle Content: How does "recovery content" gracefully become "life content"? This could involve leaning more into your cultural explorations, travel, or other hobbies, making recovery an integrated part of your past, not a constant theme of your present.
  • Setting Intentional Goals: What do *you* want your platform to achieve for you in the next 1-3 years? Is it a creative outlet? A community hub? A springboard for other projects?

Phase 3: Expanding the Framework (The Vision)

Your unique, culture-first approach has the potential to be a model for others. If you ever feel the calling, we could explore how to scale your impact.

  • A Broader Healing Framework: Could the "Julia Jyde method" of integrating culture and recovery be a template to help people recovering from different forms of disordered eating or other challenges?
  • Sustainable Platform Growth: If you choose, we can build long-term strategies for partnerships, monetization, or projects that align with your core values, ensuring your platform supports you financially as well as emotionally.
