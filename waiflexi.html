Materials & Choices

Every metal, stone, and detail selected with intention and meaning

Primary Metals

Platinum

Source: Ethically sourced from North American mines

Purity: 95% platinum, 5% ruthenium for strength

Why we chose it: Hypoallergenic properties essential for Lexi's acidic skin. Symbolic strength and permanence for Saturn Return milestone. Natural white sheen complements all skin tones.

Gemstones

London Blue Topaz

Origin: Brazil, precision cut in Idar-Oberstein, Germany

Size: 4mm x 6mm oval, 1.2 carats

Why we chose it: Associated with communication and mental clarity—perfect for Gemini rising. Its blue-gray tones evoke Saturn's mysterious energy while complementing Lexi's coloring.

Rose Quartz Cabochons

Origin: Madagascar, hand-shaped

Size: 2mm cabochons (x3)

Why we chose it: Represents heart-centered balance for double Libra. Soft, nurturing energy to counter Saturn's strict lessons. Rounded cabochon form references olive shapes.

The Ring Diary

A narrative journey of creation and personal mythology

Chapter 1

The Consultation

Lexi arrived at the studio on a crisp March afternoon, her Gemini rising immediately apparent in her curious questions and quick wit. She brought not just ideas, but an entire cosmology—charts, notes, and a deep understanding of this pivotal astrological moment.

"I don't just want a pretty ring," she explained, fingers tracing imaginary patterns in the air. "I want something that feels like a tool for navigation. My Saturn Return is asking me to build structures that can actually hold my life, not just perform stability."

We spent hours discussing her double Libra need for beauty and harmony, her Gemini rising's intellectual restlessness, and how these energies were playing out during her Saturn in Pisces—a placement demanding spiritual surrender while building practical foundations.

When she mentioned her Border Collie's intelligent gaze and the strategic precision of cooking with olives, I knew we weren't just designing jewelry—we were weaving personal mythology into metal.

Chapter 2

The Design Breakthrough

The challenge was substantial: how to visually represent the integration of Libra structure with Gemini fluidity, all while honoring the transformative pressure of Saturn Return.

For days, I sketched symmetrical designs that felt too rigid, then fluid forms that lacked the foundational strength Lexi needed. The breakthrough came during a walk with my own dog, watching how she moved between focused direction and playful exploration.

What if the ring wasn't divided into clear sides, but rather transitioned gradually from structure to flow? What if Saturn's rings became both literal motif and metaphorical container for this transformation?

When I presented the three concepts, Lexi's eyes locked on Design C immediately. "This one understands that balance isn't static," she said. "It's a dynamic equilibrium."

Chapter 3

The First Prototype

Holding the first wax model was revelatory. The asymmetric balance worked beautifully in theory, but in three dimensions, it came alive with unexpected elegance.

Lexi, ever the strategist, had specific feedback about wearability. "I need to be able to type and cook without this feeling cumbersome," she noted, her practical Virgo Venus showing through. We discussed reducing the profile, softening edges, and ensuring the personal engravings would feel like secret knowledge rather than decorative elements.

When I suggested the Border Collie silhouette inside the band, her face transformed. "That's it," she whispered. "That's the piece I didn't know was missing."

The olive branches came later—a spontaneous addition while reviewing Mediterranean symbolism. Sometimes the most meaningful details emerge through conversation rather than planning.

Chapter 4

Stone Selection

Gemstones for astrological work require particular attention. We needed something that would honor both the challenge and gift of Saturn Return while supporting Lexi's specific planetary placements.

The London Blue Topaz emerged as the clear choice for the primary stone—its connection to communication supporting her Gemini rising, its cool clarity reflecting Saturn's wisdom, and its durability standing up to the ring's symbolic weight.

The rose quartz cabochons provided the softening counterpoint her double Libra nature craved—heart-centered energy to balance Saturn's severity, rounded forms to offset the geometric precision elsewhere in the design.

When the stones arrived from the cutter, the topaz held exactly the gray-blue depth we'd hoped for, capturing both celestial mystery and intellectual clarity.

To Be Continued...

The Journey Continues

More chapters will be added after each significant milestone—the metal casting, stone setting, final polishing, and the completion ritual. This is a living story of creation, just as Lexi's Saturn Return is a living process of becoming.

The ring is taking form, gathering intention with each decision, each adjustment, each moment of collaborative creativity. It's becoming not just an object, but a companion for her cosmic navigation.
