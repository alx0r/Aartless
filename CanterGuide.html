Part 1: For ALL Staff (Grooms, Trainers)

  • The stable manager creates a WhatsApp group (e.g., "🐴 Stable Intelligence").
  • Add all staff, trainers, and (optionally) the vet and farrier.

  • A new bag of feed

  • "Farrier coming Thursday 2pm"
  • "Storm warning tomorrow"

  • Training observations
  • Health concerns

Reflexive Statement (L4)

This section provides a self-critique of the system and its methodology for transparency.

On Design Philosophy

This system (Canter™ on Untitled Format) exists to centralize knowledge in a single, accessible document and resist having data locked in proprietary, expensive apps. We believe stable management systems should augment human expertise, not replace it. Canter™ handles the routine data processing so staff can focus on what matters most—horse care.

On AI Collaboration

This document is a "living document" co-created by humans and AI. Humans provide raw data (WhatsApp messages) and strategic direction. The AI (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) provides structural synthesis and updates the HTML. This introduces a "managerial bias"—the quality of the AI's output depends on the quality of the manager's prompt and the data provided.

Known Limitations

  • Requires Cultural Shift: Staff MUST be trained to post *everything* to the WhatsApp group. If they don't, the system fails.
  • Connectivity Dependent: The AI synthesis and GitHub upload require an internet connection. This document, once loaded, is offline-ready, but *updating* it is an online-only task.
  • Centralized Update Bottleneck: The update process relies on one person (the manager) running the AI prompt. A more advanced system would automate this via a server, but that would sacrifice the "free and simple" design.
  • AI Accuracy: The AI can misunderstand sarcasm, typos, or ambiguous messages. Clear, direct communication in the WhatsApp group is essential.
  • Manual Translation: The multi-language support requires manual (or AI-assisted) translation and file management. It does not update translations automatically when the English file changes.
